# 0.1% of Polymarket Accounts Take 67% of All Profits

## Wall Street Journal Investigation

**Scope:** 1.6 million Polymarket accounts dating back to November 2022

**Key findings:**
- **0.1% of accounts** captured **67% of all profits**
- Over **70% of users** lost money
- Bottom 10% of traders: average losses of **$4,000 each**
- Typical users: losing between $1–$100

## Concurrent Academic Study Findings

A concurrent academic study found:
- **68.8% of Polymarket users** lost money
- Top **1%** captured **77% of gains**

## Kalshi's Response

Kalshi spokesperson Elisabeth Diana:
- **2.9 unprofitable users** for every profitable one (recent monthly data)
- Called this typical across financial markets
- Claims more Kalshi users profit compared to day traders or sports bettors

Analysis of 35,000+ Kalshi "mention" markets: average "yes" bettors lose **11% of wagered amounts** — worse returns than Las Vegas slot machines.

## Professionalization Trend

The Journal reported prediction markets are increasingly dominated by sophisticated players:
- Quantitative firms operate algorithms
- Purchase premium data feeds
- Execute tens of thousands of trades daily

**Notable institutional participants:**
- **Susquehanna International Group**: reportedly trading hundreds of millions weekly
- **Jump Trading**: active in prediction markets
