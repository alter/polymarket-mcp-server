# How AI is helping retail traders exploit prediction market 'glitches'

## Core Strategy

A fully automated trading bot executed 8,894 trades on short-term crypto prediction markets, generating approximately $150,000 in profits. The bot targeted moments when "Yes" and "No" contracts on five-minute Bitcoin and Ethereum markets traded below their theoretical $1 combined value.

## The Mechanics

The strategy exploited what should be a pricing certainty: "Yes" and "No" outcomes on any binary prediction contract must sum to exactly $1. When combined prices dipped to $0.97, traders could purchase both sides and lock in a three-cent profit at settlement. This translates to roughly $16.80 per trade, yielding returns of 1.5% to 3% per execution when deploying approximately $1,000 per round-trip.

## Market Conditions

Polymarket's five-minute Bitcoin prediction contracts typically show only $5,000–$15,000 in order-book depth per side during active sessions. This liquidity constraint prevents larger institutional traders from deploying substantial capital without significantly impacting prices and eliminating their profit margins.

## Broader Implications

The article identifies a shift from human-driven prediction markets toward algorithmic trading dominance. As systems increasingly arbitrage prediction markets against options and derivatives pricing, "prediction markets risk becoming mirrors of the derivatives market rather than independent signals."

## Competitive Dynamics

Once inefficiencies become widely known, competition intensifies as additional bots target the same opportunities. This drives spreads tighter and eventually erodes profitability, making speed and latency increasingly decisive factors for success.

## Scale of Bot Dominance

A simple review of Polymarket's public leaderboard found that 14 of the 20 most profitable wallets are bots. The paper "Unravelling the Probabilistic Forest" (August 2025) estimates that arbitrage traders extracted roughly $40 million from Polymarket between April 2024 and April 2025.
