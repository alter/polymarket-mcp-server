# From Swap Axioms to Weighted Geometric Means: A Characterization of AMMs

**arXiv ID:** 2604.16898

## Overview

This paper provides an axiomatic characterization of AMMs based on swap axioms, showing that weighted geometric means (Balancer-style) emerge naturally from reasonable axioms. This connects to the broader program of understanding what makes a "good" AMM, which has direct implications for prediction market AMM design.

## Relevance to Prediction Markets

The axiomatic approach to characterizing AMMs (which this paper represents along with arXiv:2302.00196 and arXiv:2210.00048) is critical for prediction market mechanism design:
- Understanding which properties are necessary vs. sufficient for good market-making
- Connecting DeFi AMM design to the information elicitation goals of prediction markets
- Providing theoretical grounding for new AMM designs targeting prediction markets
