# Kelly Criterion Revisited: Optimal Bets

**Authors:** Edward W. Piotrowski and Malgorzata Schroeder  
**arXiv ID:** physics/0607166  
**Submitted:** July 18, 2006  
**Published:** European Physical Journal B (2007)  
**Presented at:** APFA5 Conference, Torino, 2006

## Abstract

The paper examines "Kelly criterion, that maximizes the expectation value of the logarithm of wealth for bookmaker bets." The authors leverage projective symmetries to explain why this approach outperforms alternative betting strategies. They also connect Kelly's methodology to "an interesting financial interpretation of the Boltzmann/Shannon entropy" and propose a "no-go" hypothesis concerning large-scale investors.

## Key Contributions

- Use of projective symmetries to explain Kelly optimality
- Novel connection between Kelly betting and Boltzmann/Shannon entropy
- "No-go" hypothesis about large-scale investors (why Kelly may not scale to very large positions)

## Significance

This paper provides a physics-based perspective on why Kelly betting is optimal, connecting it to entropy and symmetry principles — the same mathematical structures underlying information theory.
