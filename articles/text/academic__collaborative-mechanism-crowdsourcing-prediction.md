# A Collaborative Mechanism for Crowdsourcing Prediction Problems

## Abstract

The authors introduce a novel approach to crowdsourced prediction tasks that improves upon competition-based methods like the Netflix Prize. Their framework, termed a "Crowdsourced Learning Mechanism," leverages prediction market principles where participants collectively refine a hypothesis. The system publishes current predictions and allows contributors to propose updates by wagering on improvements.

## Key Findings

- Traditional machine learning competitions have weaknesses in their incentive mechanisms for participants
- The proposed mechanism combines collaborative learning with prediction market concepts
- Participants are financially rewarded proportionally to the performance gains their contributions generate — a participant will profit an amount that scales according to how much her update improves performance on a released test set
- The approach was presented as an extended abstract at NIPS 2011
