# Polymarket's Trading Volume May Be 25% Fake, Columbia Study Finds

**Study Title:** "Network-Based Detection of Wash Trading"  
**Authors:** Allen Sirolly, Hongyao Ma, Yash Kanoria, Rajiv Sethi (Columbia University)  
**Published:** November 6, 2025  
**Source:** CoinDesk

## Core Finding

Approximately **one-quarter of all trades** executed on Polymarket between 2022 and 2025 were likely wash trades — users rapidly buying and selling contracts (often to themselves or colluding accounts) to inflate activity metrics without changing their net market position.

## Volume Inflation Scale

- Peak: Nearly **60% of weekly volume** in December 2024 was flagged as likely wash trading
- Some weeks saw over **90% of trades** in election and sports markets flagged as suspicious
- **Overall estimated wash trading rate:** ~25% of historical volume

## Most Affected Market Categories

- **Sports markets:** 45% of all-time volume likely due to wash trading
- **Election markets:** 17% likely wash trading
- **Crypto markets:** 3% likely wash trading
- **Peak weeks:** Election markets reached 95% flagged activity (week of March 24, 2025); sports markets hit 90% (week of October 21, 2024)

## Detection Methodology

Novel algorithm examining:
- How often users open and quickly close positions
- Trading primarily with accounts exhibiting similar patterns
- Complex networks forming trading loops or clusters
- Capital reuse across multiple wallets

One identified cluster: **43,000+ wallets** responsible for nearly **$1 million in trading volume**, mostly at prices under a penny.

## Suspected Motivations

Many suspected wash trading wallets made no real profits, suggesting the goal was to **game future incentives like token airdrops or platform rankings** rather than financial return.

## Platform Vulnerabilities

- Polymarket does not require identity verification
- Charges no trading fees
- Both factors make it especially vulnerable to wash trading

## Earlier 2024 Findings (Pre-Columbia Study)

Before this study, analysts at crypto research firms Chaos Labs and Inca Digital found evidence of rampant wash trading during the 2024 US elections:
- **Chaos Labs:** Wash trading constituted around one-third of trading volume on Polymarket's presidential market
- **Inca Digital:** Actual volume was about $1.75 billion vs. Polymarket's reported $2.7 billion
- Chaos Labs attributed discrepancy to Polymarket conflating traded shares with dollars

## Recommendations

Researchers recommended Polymarket implement their detection approach to identify and remove suspicious wallets, stressing that wash trading "skews market data and undermines confidence" in prediction market accuracy.
