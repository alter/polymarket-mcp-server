## Strategy 2: Structural / Market-Making

Crypto markets operated differently — traders acted as market makers rather than directional bettors.

Key findings:
- Three addresses ran "symmetric order placement" bots: simultaneously buying both increase and decrease outcomes
- One address: **94% of trades were symmetric orders** with sub-$6 median positions
- Profit model: maker rebates + liquidity premiums (not prediction accuracy)

---

## Strategy 3: Research-Driven / Cognitive Edge

Low-frequency traders (~2–3 positions per month) leveraging specialized knowledge:

**Weather forecasting:**
- Used public meteorological data
- Only entered when win probability exceeded 0.77

**FDV market arbitrage:**
- Identified mispriced assets
- Bought NO at "50–55 cents, collecting $1 at settlement"

---

## Core Insight

Success required clarity about **which game you're playing** before optimizing execution:
- Direction traders: optimize position sizing, market selection
- Market makers: optimize automation, latency, spread capture
- Research traders: optimize information sourcing, domain expertise

The three strategies require completely different tooling, mindset, and risk management.
