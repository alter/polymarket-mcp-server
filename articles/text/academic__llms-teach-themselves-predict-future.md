# LLMs Can Teach Themselves to Better Predict the Future

## Abstract

The researchers present a self-improvement framework for large language models focused on forecasting. Their approach uses model self-play to generate pairs of diverse reasoning trajectories that are then ranked by accuracy against real outcomes. The method applies Direct Preference Optimization fine-tuning without requiring human-curated training data.

## Key Findings

- Testing on Phi-4 14B and DeepSeek-R1 14B models showed prediction accuracy improvements of 7-10% compared to baseline versions
- The enhanced models achieved forecasting performance on par with forecasting capabilities of much larger frontier models like GPT-4o
- The framework successfully enables models to improve their future prediction abilities through self-directed learning
