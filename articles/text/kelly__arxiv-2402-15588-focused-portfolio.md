# Sizing the Bets in a Focused Portfolio

**Authors:** Vuko Vukcevic and Robert Keser  
**arXiv ID:** 2402.15588  
**Submitted:** February 23, 2024  
**Subject area:** Quantitative Finance — Portfolio Management and Computational Finance

## Abstract

The paper presents a mathematical framework for focused investing strategies advocated by prominent investors like Warren Buffett and Charlie Munger. The investor's role is to think about probabilities of different outcomes for a set of businesses. Based on these assumptions, the tool calculates the optimal allocation of capital.

## Key Features

The model employs a generalized Kelly Criterion with multiple constraints:
- Prohibition on short selling
- Limited leverage options
- Maximum risk threshold for permanent capital loss
- Individual position size limits

## Notable Contribution

The paper applies the software to an example portfolio and draws observations regarding "excessive diversification," providing practitioners with a publicly available tool for capital allocation in concentrated portfolios.

## Relevance

This paper shows how to adapt the Kelly criterion for a focused (concentrated) portfolio with realistic constraints, directly applicable to traders who want to maximize growth on a small number of high-conviction prediction market positions.
