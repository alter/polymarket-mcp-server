# Crowdsourced Outcome Determination in Prediction Markets

## Abstract

The paper addresses how decentralized prediction markets can determine outcomes through voting by arbiters who may have financial stakes in the market. The researchers derive conditions under which we can incentivize arbiters to vote truthfully by using funds raised from market fees to implement a peer prediction mechanism.

## Key Findings

- The mechanism solves a critical problem: prediction markets require outcome verification, but decentralized versions lack a trusted central authority
- The authors develop incentive structures that encourage honest voting despite arbiters' potential conflicts of interest
- The approach leverages market fees to fund a peer prediction mechanism that rewards truthful reporting
- The research includes analysis of practical parameter values for real-world implementation
