# The 5 Most Consistent Betfair Trading Strategies for Long-Term Profit

## Core Distinction
Betfair trading differs from traditional betting — traders operate "like a stock trader, buying (backing) and selling (laying) positions" rather than predicting outcomes. Success requires: proven strategies, disciplined risk management, and sound psychology.

## Strategy 1: Scalping
**Mechanics:** Place back and lay bets on same selection within seconds to profit from minimal odds movements (one or two "ticks").
**Entry/Exit:** Identify walls of money in market depth using trading software. Exit immediately once opposing bet is matched.
**Risk:** Never allow a small scalp to become a large position.
**Markets:** Final 10-15 minutes before horse races, liquid pre-match football.
**Profitability:** High-volume, low-margin: "the small, consistent wins will outweigh the small, managed losses."

## Strategy 2: Lay The Draw (LTD)
**Mechanics:** Lay bets against the draw in football, profiting when a goal is scored and draw odds increase sharply.
**Entry/Exit:** Select matches with likely goal-scoring. Exit immediately after the first goal.
**Risk:** Set time-based or price-based stop-losses. Avoid holding if match remains 0-0 beyond predetermined point.
**Edge Source:** "Time decay" and "the market's overreaction to a goal."

## Strategy 3: Pre-Match Odds Fluctuation
**Mechanics:** Back or lay selections before events begin, profiting from predictable odds movements driven by team news.
**Back-to-Lay (B2L):** Back anticipating odds will shorten, then lay at lower prices.
**Lay-to-Back (L2B):** Lay expecting odds will drift, then back at higher prices.
**Risk:** If backing at 2.5 expecting 2.3, exit if odds drift to 2.7.
**Best Markets:** Horse racing (jockey/ground info), football (team announcements), tennis (fitness updates).

## Strategy 4: In-Play 'Under Goals' Trading
**Mechanics:** Back "Under 2.5 Goals" during matches, profiting as odds decrease during goal-free periods (time decay).
**Entry/Exit:** Enter at match start or during quiet periods. Exit by laying at lower odds after predetermined time.
**Risk:** Goal scoring causes odds to spike upward.
**Edge:** Exploits "steadily decrease" in odds as scoreless time accumulates.

## Universal Risk Management
- Risk only 1–2% of bankroll per trade
- Define stop-losses before entering positions
- Maintain a written trading plan
- Keep detailed records of all trades

## Psychology
Success requires "discipline, patience, and objectivity." Greed and fear are destructive. Consistency = reliable execution with positive expected value across many trades, not winning every trade.
