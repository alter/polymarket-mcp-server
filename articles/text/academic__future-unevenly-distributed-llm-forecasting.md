# Future Is Unevenly Distributed: Forecasting Ability of LLMs Depends on What We're Asking

## Abstract

The researchers examine how well Large Language Models can predict real-world events beyond their training cutoff. They found that forecasting ability varies sharply with domain structure and prompt framing. The study evaluates different model families on social, political, and economic questions, analyzing how context, question type, and external knowledge influence prediction accuracy.

## Key Findings

1. Variable Performance — LLM predictive capability is inconsistent across different domains and question formats
2. Context Matters — How questions are framed significantly impacts forecasting results
3. Knowledge Integration — Adding factual news context changes how models form beliefs and affects failure patterns
4. Domain Sensitivity — Performance depends heavily on the specific subject matter being predicted

The study collected approximately 10,000 forecasting questions from various prediction markets such as Polymarket, Metaculus, and Manifold Markets.
