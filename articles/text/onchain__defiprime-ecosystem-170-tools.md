# Polymarket Ecosystem: 170+ Tools Across 19 Categories

Polymarket has evolved into the world's largest prediction market platform, with ~$9 billion valuation following a $2 billion investment from Intercontinental Exchange. The ecosystem comprises 170+ third-party tools across 19 categories.

**Key metrics:**
- Trading Volume (2025): $44 billion
- Developer Grants distributed: $2.5 million+
- Monthly Active Volume via Betmoar: ~5% of Polymarket's monthly activity
- Documented Bot Returns: One AI system generated $2.2 million in profits in two months
- One documented bot turned $313 into $414,000 in a single month via temporal arbitrage
