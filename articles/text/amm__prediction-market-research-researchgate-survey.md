# Prediction Markets, Automated Market Makers, and Decentralized Finance (DeFi)

**Source:** ResearchGate

## Overview

Survey paper connecting the prediction market AMM literature (LMSR, LS-LMSR) with the DeFi AMM literature (constant product, constant mean). Examines the evolution from prediction-market-specific AMMs to general DeFi primitives.

## Key Findings

- LMSR was the original AMM, designed specifically for prediction markets with bounded loss and proper scoring
- LS-LMSR improves on LMSR by making liquidity adaptive, enabling profit in favorable market conditions
- Constant product market makers (Uniswap) dominate DeFi due to simplicity and gas efficiency
- LS-LMSR has advantages over constant product for prediction markets but requires complex computation
- The DeFi and prediction market AMM traditions are converging (see arXiv:2302.00196)

## Significance

Good survey-level reference connecting two research traditions (academic prediction market theory and practical DeFi engineering) that evolved largely independently before 2020.
