# Kelly Bets and Single-Letter Codes: Optimal Information Processing in Natural Systems

**Authors:** Alexander S. Moffett and Andrew W. Eckford  
**arXiv ID:** 2104.14277  
**Submitted:** April 29, 2021 (revised June 15, 2025)  
**Classification:** Computer Science > Information Theory  
**Published:** IEEE Transactions on Molecular, Biological, and Multi-Scale Communications

## Abstract Overview

The researchers explore connections between Kelly betting strategy and information theory. Their key finding is that "Kelly bets are closely related to optimal single-letter codes" and can achieve rate-distortion bounds perfectly. This suggests biological systems operating under computational constraints might perform information processing optimally.

## Core Contributions

The work establishes that:
- Rate-distortion tradeoffs in investment scenarios follow "a simple linear bound" that becomes achievable when single-letter codes are optimal
- Expanding an organism's available strategies enables optimal performance across multiple environmental conditions
- Increasing phenotypic diversity correlates with improved growth rates under specific conditions

## Connection to Kelly

This paper deepens the information-theoretic interpretation of Kelly betting: Kelly bets are not just analogous to channel coding — they are mathematically equivalent to optimal single-letter source codes. The mutual information between signal and outcome directly determines the Kelly-optimal bet size and expected growth rate.

## Relevance

For prediction market traders: the "information" you have about an outcome relative to the market's information is exactly what drives your Kelly edge. The better calibrated your probability estimates (lower divergence from truth), the larger your sustainable Kelly fraction.
