# Kalshi Institutional Entry & Market Overview 2025–2026

## Institutional Participation
- **SIG (Susquehanna International Group)**: Kalshi's first official market maker — reduced fees and higher position limits
- **Jump Trading**: One of the earliest prop firms active on prediction markets
- **DRW**: Building dedicated prediction market desks
- **Citadel Securities CEO Peng Zhao**: Personally invested in Kalshi's $185M Series C (June 2025, $5B valuation)

## Market Volume
- Prediction market volume on Kalshi hit $43.1 billion in 2025
- As of 2026: Kalshi reportedly valued at $11B
- Weekly trading: ~$2.7B on Kalshi, ~$2.1B on Polymarket global
- Together: $10–13B traded monthly in US prediction markets
- Projected: $1 trillion annually by 2030 (Eilers & Krejcik Gaming)

## New Product Directions (2026)
- **Perpetual futures contracts**: Starting with crypto, providing regulated alternative to offshore perpetuals for US traders
- **Art auction prediction markets**: Traders can bet on high-end art sales prices, auction results, total transaction values

## API & Technology
- Kalshi REST + WebSocket API publicly available
- Rate limits: 20 req/sec reads (basic tier), 100 req/sec (premier tier)
- Tokenized markets on Solana (new — on-chain asset representation)
- Centralized order book (traditional exchange model)

## Regulatory Status
- CFTC Designated Contract Market since 2020
- Available in all 50 US states
- Contracts classified as derivatives under federal law
- KYC required; US-focused user base

## Speed & Arbitrage Windows
- Opportunity windows collapsed from 12.3 seconds (2024) to 2.7 seconds (early 2026)
- 73% of prediction market arbitrage profits captured by sub-100ms bots
