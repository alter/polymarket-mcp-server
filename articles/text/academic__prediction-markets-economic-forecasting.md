# Prediction Markets for Economic Forecasting

## Abstract

The paper examines prediction markets and their application to economic forecasting. The authors demonstrate that these markets possess several advantages over traditional forecasting methods. Prepared for the Handbook of Economic Forecasting, Volume 2.

## Key Findings

- Prediction markets quickly incorporate new information, are largely efficient, and impervious to manipulation
- They generally outperform professional forecasters and polls in terms of statistical accuracy
- Markets can reveal the underlying economic models used in forecasts
- Markets serve as tools for testing existing economic models
- Evidence drawn from political contests, sporting events, and economic outcomes

NBER Working Paper No. 18222, July 2012.
