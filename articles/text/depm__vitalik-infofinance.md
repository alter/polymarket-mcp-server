# From prediction markets to info finance

**Author:** Vitalik Buterin  
**Published:** November 9, 2024  
**URL:** https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2024/11/09/infofinance.html

## Overview

Vitalik Buterin explores why prediction markets fascinate him beyond their obvious betting appeal. He argues that prediction markets serve as "a very useful tool for the world" in their current form, but represent just one example of a broader category he terms "info finance."

## Prediction Markets' Current Value

Buterin highlights how Polymarket functioned effectively during the 2024 US election, showing "direct truth" about probabilities when other news sources hedged or offered false hope. The platform demonstrated accuracy in real-time outcome assessment.

## The Larger Vision: Info Finance

Beyond elections, Buterin proposes that info finance mechanisms could revolutionize "social media, science, news, governance, and other fields." Rather than viewing prediction markets narrowly as gambling, he positions them as infrastructure for collective intelligence and better decision-making.

The key insight: the market is the "engine," and some other non-financialized trustworthy mechanism is the "steering wheel."

## Core Insight: Markets as Information Aggregators

The fundamental appeal lies in how markets aggregate distributed information. When properly designed, financial incentives encourage participants to reveal accurate beliefs about uncertain outcomes. This mechanism could extend far beyond sports betting or political forecasting.

## Futarchy Connection

Buterin credits Robin Hanson's work on futarchy as foundational inspiration, acknowledging how market-based governance models might improve institutional decision-making more broadly.

## Ethereum Governance Applications (2026 Context)

In early 2026, Buterin urged a hard reset of Ethereum governance, pushing a two-layer model that fuses prediction markets with anonymous, non-token voting:
- Layer 1: "maximally open and maximizes accountability (it's a market, anyone can buy and sell, if you make good decisions you win money if you make bad decisions you lose money)" — a decentralized executive
- Layer 2: "decentralized and pluralistic, and that maximizes space for intrinsic motivation" — cannot be token-based

## Personal Prediction Market Activity

Buterin disclosed making roughly $70,000 on Polymarket using a principal of about $440,000, using an "anti-insanity mode" approach focused on markets dominated by irrational excitement.
