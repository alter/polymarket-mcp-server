# On Convergence of Forecasts in Prediction Markets

## Abstract

The paper provides a mathematical study of convergence of forecasts elicited from prediction markets to true probabilistic characteristics, such as probabilities of random events or expected values of random variables. The authors consider a dynamic game in which agents predict the values of a sequence of random vectors.

## Key Findings

- The key novelty is showing that convergence to true conditional expectations takes place regardless of strategies used by agents — the only assumption being that the market contains agents who make correct forecasts
- The paper establishes that market aggregation mechanisms can effectively extract accurate collective forecasts when skilled predictors participate
- Convergence occurs toward the next-period conditional expectations of future random vectors
