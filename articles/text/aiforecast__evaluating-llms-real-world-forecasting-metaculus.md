# Evaluating LLMs on Real-World Forecasting Against Expert Forecasters

**Author:** Janna Lu

**arXiv:** 2507.04562 | Submitted: July 6, 2025 (v3: August 4, 2025)

## Summary

Evaluates state-of-the-art LLMs on 464 Metaculus forecasting questions, comparing against human crowds and expert forecasters. Key finding: frontier LLMs beat human crowds but still significantly lag behind expert-level forecasters.

## Key Methods

- 464 Metaculus forecasting questions
- Frontier-class LLMs tested
- Comparison against human crowd and expert forecasters
- Brier score as primary metric
- Two quarters of Metaculus forecasting tournament data

## Key Results

- Frontier LLMs: exceed typical human crowd accuracy
- Frontier LLMs: significantly underperform expert forecasters
- Performance gap between LLMs and experts remains meaningful despite year-over-year improvement
- Provides calibrated comparison across crowd / LLM / expert tiers

## Relevance to Polymarket Trading

Calibrates expectations: LLMs beat the casual crowd (Polymarket average trader) but not superforecasters. Since most Polymarket participants are not superforecasters, an LLM agent with proper calibration should have a positive edge against market prices in aggregate.
