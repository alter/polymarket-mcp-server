## Overview

In May 2026, Wintermute — a crypto market-making firm processing over $3.5 trillion in annual trading volume across 70+ exchanges — confirmed it is providing two-sided liquidity across prediction market platforms including Polymarket and Kalshi.

---

## Key Details

### What Wintermute Is Doing
- Quoting two-sided markets on event contracts
- **Not placing directional bets** — purely market making (spread capture)
- Creating a mechanical link between Polymarket and Kalshi via cross-platform liquidity flows
- Capital dynamically flowing between platforms to tighten spreads and absorb larger trades

### Why (Jake Ostrovskis, Head of OTC Trading)
> "Prediction markets have the demand profile of a major asset class but the liquidity profile of an early-stage one. For these markets to become a reliable real-time source of probability estimates, they need sustained two-sided liquidity."

> "That depth tightens spreads, supports larger trade sizes, and in turn improves the signal embedded in market prices."

### Structural Advantage
- Polymarket settles in USDC on public blockchain → gives Wintermute custody and execution edge vs. traditional TradFi market makers
- Kalshi (CFTC-regulated, USD) + Polymarket (offshore, USDC) requires navigating different regulatory frameworks — advantage for well-capitalized firms

## Implications for Arbitrage and Spread Capture

1. **Bid-ask spreads will narrow** in high-volume markets (Wintermute's primary effect)
2. **Cross-platform arbitrage harder:** Mechanical link between venues reduces price divergence
3. **Latency arbitrage harder:** Professional market makers quote faster than retail arbers
4. **Opportunity migration:** Smaller/exotic markets, less liquid categories may retain more spread

### Historical Analogy
The IMDEA study documented ~$40M in arbitrage extracted between April 2024–2025. With institutional MMs now present, this rate should compress substantially for simple strategies.
