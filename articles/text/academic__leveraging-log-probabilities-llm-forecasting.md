# Leveraging Log Probabilities in Language Models to Forecast Future Events

## Abstract

The authors present a method for using Large Language Models to predict future events. They describe their approach as employing data on current trends and their trajectories for generating forecasts on 15 different topics. The predictions are calculated using a multi-step process centered on log probabilities.

## Key Findings

- Achieved a Brier score of 0.186, representing a 26% improvement over random chance and a 19% improvement compared to widely-available AI systems
- Demonstrates that log probability signals from LLMs carry useful predictive information
