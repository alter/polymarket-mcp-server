# Prediction Markets Sports Contracts: Kalshi vs Polymarket Research 2025-2026

## Market Scale (2025 Data)

- Kalshi + Polymarket: **97.5% of prediction market share**
- Kalshi 2025 trading volume: **$238 billion** (+1,100% YoY)
- Polymarket 2025: **$220 billion**
- Q1 2026: Kalshi $33B, Polymarket $26.17B

## Sports as Dominant Category

- Sports = **80% of total trading volume on Kalshi**
- Sports + politics + crypto = 91% of Kalshi volume, 90% of Polymarket volume
- Kalshi volume: ~75% US sports (NFL, NBA, MLB)

## Fee Structure Comparison

| Platform | Sports fee | Formula |
|---|---|---|
| Kalshi | 0.07 × shares × p × (1-p) | Peaks at $0.50 contracts |
| Polymarket | 0.03 × shares × p × (1-p) | Cheaper for sports |

Kalshi sports fee = 2.3× Polymarket sports fee. For volume players, platform choice matters enormously.

## Contract Pricing Mechanics

- Binary outcome: $0–$1, price = implied probability
- Buy 100 YES at $0.40 → receive $100 if outcome occurs
- Winning position worth $1.00 at resolution

## Academic Finding: Favourite-Longshot Bias on Kalshi

CEPR analysis of 300,000+ contracts:
- **Low-price contracts** win far less often than needed to break even
- **High-price contracts** win more often → small positive returns for buyers
- Takers lose **almost 32% on average**; makers average loss ~10%

"Evidence of a weakening in the favourite-longshot bias" in 2025 data — market sophistication growing.

## Kalshi Combos (Parlay-Style)

- Launched mid-December 2025; self-certified with CFTC
- First week: **>$100 million** in volume
- April 2026 record: **$412.5M/week** (10.6% of Kalshi total)

Polymarket also self-certified "Combinatoric Athletic Outcome Contract" with CFTC.

## Regulatory Landscape

- Robinhood launched event contracts (March 2025) via Kalshi infrastructure — 24M+ funded accounts
- Polymarket re-entered US mid-2025 via QCEX acquisition
- By end-2025: 3 CFTC-regulated venues offering US sports event contracts
- State challenges: Massachusetts, Michigan, Nevada, New Jersey, Ohio

## Valuations (May 2026)

- Kalshi Series F: **$22 billion**
- Polymarket funding talks: **$15 billion**

## Transferable Insights

1. Sports is the highest-volume, most liquid prediction market category globally
2. Favourite-longshot bias is weaker on Kalshi sports than other categories — but still present
3. Polymarket offers cheaper fees for sports (0.03 vs 0.07) — significant for high-volume strategies
4. Combo/parlay markets growing rapidly — new structural edges in correlated outcome pricing
5. Platform maturation rapidly attracts professional market makers, compressing edges
