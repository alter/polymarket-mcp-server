# Optimal Automated Market Makers: Differentiable Economics and Strong Duality

**Authors:** Michael J. Curry, Zhou Fan, David C. Parkes

**arXiv ID:** 2402.09129

## Abstract

The role of a market maker is to simultaneously offer to buy and sell quantities of goods, often a financial asset such as a share, at specified prices. The literature on AMM design has mainly focused on prediction markets with the goal of information elicitation, while more recent work motivated by DeFi has focused on profit maximization. This paper shows that finding an optimal market maker is dual to an optimal transport problem with geometric constraints. The work demonstrates that optimal mechanisms for multiple goods can take advantage of bundling, including improved prices for bundled purchases and sometimes accepting payment "in kind." The authors employ differentiable economics methodology to generate mechanism conjectures.

## Key Contributions

- Theoretical framework establishing duality between optimal market maker design and constrained optimal transport problems
- Extension beyond single-asset settings to complex multi-good markets with bundling behavior under adverse selection
- Novel mechanisms: optimal designs can offer improved bundled pricing and accept alternative payment forms
- Methodological: demonstrates differentiable economics as a tool for guiding theoretical mechanism design investigations
