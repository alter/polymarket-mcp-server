# Forecasting Skill of a Crowd-Prediction Platform: A Comparison of Exchange Rate Forecasts

## Abstract

The study examines how well crowd predictions on Metaculus perform when forecasting exchange rates. Exchange rate crowd-predictions made on Metaculus are compared to predictions made by the random-walk, a statistical model considered extremely hard to beat.

## Key Findings

- Despite extensive research on prediction tournaments and collective forecasting, this is one of the first direct comparisons between crowd-prediction platforms and established statistical benchmarks
- The random-walk model produces more accurate forecasts overall, but the crowd-prediction platform demonstrates impressive capability
- Results present evidence in favor of the crowd-prediction at Metaculus possessing considerable forecasting skill
- Crowd-prediction can be viewed as a highly versatile and accessible forecasting tool
