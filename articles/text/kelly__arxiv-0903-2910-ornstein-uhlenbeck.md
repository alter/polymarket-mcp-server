# Application of the Kelly Criterion to Ornstein-Uhlenbeck Processes

**Authors:** Yingdong Lv and Bernhard K. Meister  
**arXiv ID:** 0903.2910 (q-fin.PM)  
**Submitted:** March 17, 2009  
**Presented at:** Complex'2009 (Shanghai, February 23-25, 2009)

## Abstract

The researchers examine "the Kelly criterion in the continuous time framework" and build upon earlier work by E.O. Thorp. Their contributions include proving the existence of an optimal investment strategy in a broad context and deriving the corresponding optimal wealth process. They deliver "a simple formula for calculating the optimal portfolio" for certain asset price processes and analyze how this strategy functions when multiple Ornstein-Uhlenbeck processes govern asset prices, concluding with observations about market implications.

## Key Topics

- Kelly criterion in continuous-time framework
- Ornstein-Uhlenbeck (mean-reverting) processes as asset price models
- Existence and derivation of optimal wealth process
- Closed-form formula for optimal portfolio under OU dynamics
- Multiple OU processes (multiple mean-reverting assets)

## Significance

Ornstein-Uhlenbeck processes are widely used to model mean-reverting assets and spreads in quantitative finance (pairs trading, prediction market prices near boundaries, etc.). This paper provides the closed-form Kelly solution for such processes — directly applicable to prediction market strategies where prices exhibit mean-reversion around fundamental probabilities.

## Note

This is the same author (Bernhard K. Meister) as the 2024 paper on Kelly criterion applied to prediction markets (arXiv:2412.14144), establishing a long research trajectory in this domain.
