# Prediction Markets with Intermittent Contributions

## Abstract

Although both data availability and the demand for accurate forecasts are increasing, collaboration between stakeholders is often constrained by data ownership and competitive interests. This paper works within a prediction market framework, where independent agents trade forecasts of uncertain future events in exchange for rewards, accounting for historical agent performance, adapting to time-varying conditions, and permitting agents to enter and exit the market at will.

## Key Findings

- The proposed design employs robust regression models to learn optimal forecast combinations while handling missing submissions
- Introduces a payoff allocation mechanism that considers both in-sample and out-of-sample performance while satisfying several desirable economic properties
- Case studies using simulated and real-world data demonstrate the effectiveness and adaptability of the proposed market design
- Framework enables cooperation despite data ownership barriers
