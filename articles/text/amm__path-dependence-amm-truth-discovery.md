# Path Dependence in AMM-Based Markets: Mathematical Proof and Implications for Truth Discovery

**Author:** Keroshan Pillay

**arXiv ID:** 2503.00201

## Abstract

This paper demonstrates that Automated Market Maker (AMM) based markets, such as those using constant product formulas (e.g., Uniswap), are inherently path-dependent. We prove mathematically that the sequence of operations in AMMs determines the final state, challenging the notion that market prices solely reflect information. This property has profound implications for decentralized prediction markets that rely on AMMs for price discovery, as it demonstrates they cannot function as pure "truth machines." Using both mathematical proofs and empirical evidence from ETH/USDC pools, we show that AMM-based markets incorporate historical path information beyond the current market beliefs.

## Key Contributions

1. Mathematical proof that AMM operation sequences determine final market states
2. Demonstration that AMM prices reflect historical transaction paths, not merely current information
3. Empirical validation using ETH/USDC trading data
4. Challenge to the assumption that decentralized prediction markets function as "truth machines"

## Implications for Prediction Markets

AMM-based prediction markets cannot function as pure information aggregation devices. Their prices encode path history, which means the interpretation of AMM prices as probabilities requires caution. This is relevant for mechanism design of decentralized prediction markets.
