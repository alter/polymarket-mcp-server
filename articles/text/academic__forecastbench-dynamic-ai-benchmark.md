# ForecastBench: A Dynamic Benchmark of AI Forecasting Capabilities

## Abstract

The researchers created ForecastBench to evaluate machine learning systems' accuracy on standardized forecasting questions. The benchmark comprises 1,000 forecasting questions about future events with no known answers at submission time, updated regularly to prevent data leakage. Published as a conference paper at ICLR 2025.

## Key Findings

- Expert human forecasters significantly outperformed the best-performing LLM, with statistical significance (p < 0.001)
- State-of-the-art models such as Claude-3.5 Sonnet and GPT-4 Turbo perform only roughly as well as a simple median of survey forecasts from humans with no forecasting experience
- Models perform significantly worse than the median forecast of superforecasters
- Results are publicly displayed on a leaderboard at forecastbench.org
- Questions are sourced from prediction market sites (Manifold, Polymarket, Metaculus) using high levels of active human trading as a quality filter
