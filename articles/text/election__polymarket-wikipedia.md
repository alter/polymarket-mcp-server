# Polymarket - Wikipedia

## Overview

Polymarket is a cryptocurrency-based prediction market founded in 2020 by Shayne Coplan, headquartered in Manhattan. The platform allows users to wager on future outcomes using USDC cryptocurrency via the Polygon blockchain network.

## Key Statistics

The platform demonstrates significant wealth concentration:
- "0.1% of accounts net 67% of the profits on Polymarket, while more than 70% of users are losing money"
- Approximately 3% of traders drive the majority of price discovery activity

## 2024 U.S. Election Activity

The presidential race became the platform's most active market, with over $3.3 billion wagered by November 5, 2024. Polymarket predicted Biden would withdraw weeks before his announcement, though predictions on Harris's running mate proved inaccurate (68% on Josh Shapiro, but Tim Walz was chosen).

## Regulatory Status

- 2022 CFTC fine of $1.4 million for operating as an unregistered derivatives exchange
- Banned in France, Brazil, and Switzerland
- Trump administration eased regulatory scrutiny in 2025
- Donald Trump Jr. became an advisor after his firm invested in the company
- September 2025: company acquired a CFTC-registered exchange and clearinghouse to secure US operating license

## Notable Controversies

**French Whale (2024):** A French trader controlled four accounts worth approximately $30 million betting on Trump, ultimately winning $85 million upon his electoral victory.

**Insider Trading Concerns:**
- Military personnel implicated in suspicious betting patterns
- An Israeli Air Force officer allegedly provided strike timing information yielding $244,000 in profits
- Intercepted statement: "the entire [squadron] is on Polymarket, the entire air force is betting"
- US Army soldier Gannon Van Dyke charged with insider trading for betting on Maduro capture using classified information about Operation Absolute Resolve

**Journalist Harassment:** Users placed large bets on Iranian missile strikes in March 2026, then allegedly harassed Israeli journalist Emanuel Fabian of The Times of Israel, attempting to pressure him to alter reporting.

**Wash Trading:** Columbia University study (November 2025) found approximately 25% of historical trading volume involved wash trades; peaked at nearly 60% in December 2024.

**FBI Raid (November 2024):** FBI raided founder Shayne Coplan's SoHo apartment, seizing his phone. Investigation related to whether Polymarket allowed US users to access the platform illegally. No charges ultimately filed; DOJ and CFTC dropped investigations in July 2025.

## Investment and Growth

Monthly active trading users grew from 13,000 (May 2024) to 220,000 (October 2024). Platform processed over $20 billion in volume in 2025.

Notable investors include Peter Thiel's Founders Fund and Vitalik Buterin (Ethereum co-founder). Nate Silver joined as advisor in July 2024.
