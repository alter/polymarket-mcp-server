# How to Track Polymarket Whales Using Arkham Intelligence

## Overview

Polymarket operates on Polygon (Ethereum L2) with typical monthly trading volumes $8–10 billion. Since all trading data exists on a public blockchain, Arkham provides tools to analyze whale activity.

Arkham launched a complete Prediction Markets analytics suite, pulling from the same deanonymization engine built to track crypto whales. The catalogue includes **3.5 billion address labels** and **800,000 verified entities** built since 2022.

## Unique Value: Deanonymization at Scale

No other analytics tool sits on top of an identity layer that maps wallets to real-world entities at this scale. The suite effectively does to prediction market participants what Arkham already did to DeFi whales: strips away pseudonymity and replaces it with a transparent track record.

Adding prediction markets means users can see whether the same wallet that just moved $50M in Bitcoin is also sitting on a large Polymarket position.

## Method 1: Prediction Markets Dashboard

Access Arkham's Prediction Markets dashboard to view traders ranked by PNL.

Metrics available per trader:
- **Account Value**: Total USDC balance + unresolved share value
- **Active Positions**: Current real-time holdings
- **Total PNL**: Lifetime earnings/losses
- **ROI**: % returns relative to capital risked
- **Volume**: Cumulative trading activity (experience indicator)
- **Biggest Win**: Largest single profitable prediction (skill vs. luck indicator)
- **Win Rate**: % of closed profitable positions

Click through to individual trader address pages for detailed activity feeds showing buy/sell decisions with share quantities and pricing.
