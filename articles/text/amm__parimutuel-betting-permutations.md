# Parimutuel Betting on Permutations

**arXiv ID:** 0804.2288

## Overview

This paper extends parimutuel betting mechanisms to the combinatorial setting where outcomes are permutations of objects. It compares and contrasts posted price mechanisms (LMSR, Dynamic Parimutuel Market DPM) with call auction models for permutation combinatorics.

## Key Content

- Analyzes the combinatorial structure of markets where outcomes are permutations
- Compares LMSR market maker with Dynamic Parimutuel Market (DPM) in the permutation setting
- Discusses call auction models where all orders are collected and processed together
- Addresses the exponential outcome space (n! permutations) and tractability challenges

## Context

This is an early paper in the combinatorial prediction market complexity literature, predating arXiv:0802.1362 (Complexity of Combinatorial Market Makers) which proved NP-hardness of LMSR for permutation markets. Together they establish the fundamental computational hardness that motivates arXiv:2411.08972's geometric approach.
