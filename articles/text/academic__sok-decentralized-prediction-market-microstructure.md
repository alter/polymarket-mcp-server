# SoK: Market Microstructure for Decentralized Prediction Markets (DePMs)

## Abstract

The paper presents a systematic overview of decentralized prediction markets dating back to 2011. The authors note that modern DePMs like Polymarket deviate materially from earlier designs like Truthcoin and Augur v1. They introduce a modular framework with eight stages for analyzing DePMs: underlying infrastructure, market topic, share structure and pricing, market initialization, trading, market resolution, settlement, and archiving.

## Key Findings

- The review encompasses hundreds of DePM proposals spanning over a decade
- For each design module, the authors enumerate variants and analyze tradeoffs involving decentralization, expressiveness, and manipulation resistance
- The research identifies open research problems within the DePM ecosystem
- Contemporary platforms show substantial departures from foundational earlier designs
