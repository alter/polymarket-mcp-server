# 84% of Polymarket Traders Losing Money

**Researcher:** Andrey Sergeenkov (independent on-chain analyst)
**Published:** April 8, 2026 (TechFlow / KuCoin)
**Dataset:** 2.5 million Polymarket wallet addresses as of April 1, 2026

## Methodology

Tracked USDC cash flows across CTF Exchange smart contracts on Polygon, capturing token splits and merges that previous analyses missed. This is why the loss rate is higher than the December 2025 DeFi Oasis study (70%) — improved methodology captures complex multi-step operations.

## Key Statistics

- **84.1%** of traders are unprofitable
- Only **16%** of addresses achieved any level of positive returns
- Only **2%** of traders have earned over $1,000 total
- Only **0.32%** had cleared $10,000
- Only **840 wallets (0.033%)** have earned more than $100,000
- Only **0.26%** earn over $5,000 monthly

## Sustainability Issue

Among profitable traders earning $5,000+ monthly:
- **53%** disappeared after one month
- Only **2.6%** continued beyond one year

## Who Is Actually Profitable

Largest profits concentrated in wallets using:
- Automated trading strategies
- Arbitrage bots
- High-frequency trading systems
- Market-making algorithms

Retail traders who trade manually typically enter the market only after price adjustments have already occurred.

## Platform Context (at time of study)

- Polymarket securing MLB partnership rights valued up to $300 million
- Expanding retail user base

## Prior Study (December 2025 — DeFi Oasis)

Blockchain analyst DeFi Oasis analyzed 1.7 million addresses and 124 million transactions:
- **70%** of traders were unprofitable
- Fewer than **0.04%** captured over **70% of total profits** (totaling $3.7 billion)

Wall Street Journal investigation (1.6 million accounts since Nov 2022):
- **0.1% of accounts** took home **67% of all profits**
- Over 70% of users lost money
- Bottom 10%: average losses of $4,000 each

## Political Context

Arizona Democrat Yassamin Ansari called both Polymarket and Kalshi "casinos where the rich and powerful are the house and everyone else is the chips" — citing the DeFi Oasis study.

## Note

Despite 84% of traders losing money, Polymarket's price predictions have achieved over **94% accuracy** one month before results are finalized. Retail traders who lose money are essentially paying for the pricing of information.
