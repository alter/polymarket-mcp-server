# PA Beacon: Polymarket Smart Money Tracking Tool Launch

**Official launch date:** April 14, 2026

PA Beacon is a prediction market monitoring platform that organizes fragmented prediction market fund activity into a continuous signal stream through advanced filtering, address profiling, leaderboard generation, and minute-level monitoring.

---

## Core Features

### Smart Money Leaderboards (Updated Every 6 Hours)

Four smart money lists:
1. **Small-Scale Strong Performers**
2. **Stable Profits**
3. **Active Alpha**
4. **High Win Rate**

Leaderboards identify successful traders based on performance metrics across different trading styles and scales.

### Real-Time Monitoring Systems

**1. Watchlist:**
Targets key addresses for targeted smart money surveillance.

**2. Large-Scale Radar:**
Monitors broader market — covers single transactions exceeding **$20,000** across sectors:
- Sports
- Politics
- Geopolitics
- Esports

---

## Operational Statistics (As of April 14, 2026)

- **101** leaderboard generations completed
- **389** unique smart money addresses identified
- **3,760** transaction alerts generated (~$8.56 million in volume)
- **1,034** large transactions captured across 481 unique addresses (~$59.5 million total)

---

## Access

Available via Telegram channels and bot functionality for real-time monitoring and alerts.

## Context: Smart Money Tracking Tool Landscape

Other tools in this space (as of 2026):

- **Polycool** (launched June 2025): Monitors top 0.5% of smart wallets; proven win rates 57.9%–73%; PnL $11K–$75K; free Telegram bot @polycool_alerts; $632+ position threshold
- **PolyTracker** (Chrome extension): Tracks up to 20 wallet addresses; 1-minute polling; desktop notifications within 60 seconds
- **Polymarket Smart Money Analyzer** (Chrome extension): Analyzes 15 top traders on each side; shows combined P&L per outcome
- **HashDive**: "Smart Score" -100 to 100 per trader based on historical performance, open bets, consistency
- **PolyAlertHub**: Real-time alerts via email or Telegram for whale moves and market activity
- **Polysights** (launched 2025): 30+ custom metrics; AI-powered summaries; automatic real-time market activity analysis
- **Polymarket Alerts** (Android app): Fastest real-time notifications for price changes, user trades, whale trades
