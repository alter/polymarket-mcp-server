# The Rise of Blockchain-Based Prediction Markets

**Author:** Anchit Jain  
**Publication:** California Management Review (UC Berkeley)  
**Date:** September 17, 2024  
**URL:** https://cmr.berkeley.edu/2024/09/the-rise-of-blockchain-based-prediction-markets/

## Overview

This article examines decentralized prediction markets (DPMs), which leverage blockchain technology to enable users to bet on future event outcomes without central intermediaries.

## Historical Context

Prediction markets date back to 1988 with the Iowa Electronic Markets (IEM), one of the earliest platforms trading contracts based on expected vote shares in U.S. elections. IEM demonstrated that market-based systems could outperform traditional polling in accuracy.

Online platforms such as Intrade and PredictIt brought prediction markets to the internet, but many faced shutdowns due to unclear regulations. These showed how fragile centralized platforms can be when legal clarity is lacking.

## How Decentralized Prediction Markets Function

Users purchase tokens representing different possible outcomes, with token prices reflecting market consensus about likelihood. When events conclude, oracles supply results to smart contracts, which distribute pooled funds to winning token holders.

## Key Advantages

1. **Transparency:** All transactions and outcomes are transparent and tamper-proof, reducing fraud risks inherent in traditional betting
2. **Market-driven odds:** Rather than bookmakers setting odds unilaterally, odds are determined purely by market dynamics
3. **Cost reduction:** Eliminating intermediaries lowers entry barriers and increases potential returns

## Polymarket Case Study (as of July 2024)

Polymarket, built on Ethereum's Polygon sidechain, featured approximately **$250 million in presidential election bets**, with political wagering comprising roughly **70% of all activity**.

## Blockchain-Specific Innovation

- **Smart contracts:** Markets operate automatically, eliminating need for central intermediaries
- **Decentralized Prediction Markets were first described in Ethereum's vision paper (late 2013)**
- Decentralized prediction platforms began appearing around 2017

## Future Directions

- Enhanced liquidity solutions through AMMs
- Advanced oracle systems for reliable data feeds
- Regulatory frameworks for mainstream adoption
- Interoperability improvements across chains
- Diversified market types covering climate, pandemics, societal trends
