# Do Prediction Markets Forecast Cryptocurrency Volatility? Evidence from Kalshi Macro Contracts

**Authors:** Hardhik Mohanty, Bhaskar Krishnamachari
**Submission Date:** April 1, 2026
**arXiv:** 2604.01431

## Abstract

Examines whether daily probability shifts in Kalshi prediction markets can predict cryptocurrency volatility. Study spans ten market event series and six digital assets from January 2023 through March 2026.

## Key Findings

**Monetary Policy Channel:**
- KXFED contracts (Fed rate expectations) demonstrate strong in-sample predictive capacity for Bitcoin volatility (t-statistic = 3.63, p < 0.001)
- Signal sensitive to 2024–2025 rate-cutting environment

**Recession Risk Signal:**
- KXRECSSNBER recession probability indicator more robust out-of-sample
- Superior stability; MSFE ratio of 0.979 (Clark-West p = 0.020)

**Inflation Channel:**
- KXCPI contracts predict altcoin volatility across multiple assets including Ethereum, Solana, Cardano, and Chainlink (t-statistics range: -2.1 to -3.4)
- Ethereum out-of-sample gains: MSFE = 0.959 (p = 0.010); Solana at p = 0.048

**Information Content:**
- Bitcoin-Fed and Chainlink-CPI relationships survive multiple-testing correction
- Signals contain unique information not reflected in Fed Funds futures, Treasury yields, or Deribit implied volatility index

## Relevance to Prediction-Market Pricing

Demonstrates that macro prediction market prices (Kalshi) contain unique information content for cryptocurrency volatility — relevant for Polymarket strategies involving crypto-related markets. The out-of-sample predictive power (MSFE < 1) confirms practical trading utility.
