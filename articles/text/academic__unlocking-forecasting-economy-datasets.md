# Unlocking the Forecasting Economy: A Suite of Datasets for the Full Lifecycle of Prediction Market

## Abstract

The researchers created the first comprehensive dataset covering decentralized prediction markets on Polymarket. Their work addresses fragmentation across blockchain and off-chain data sources by building a unified relational data system that integrates three canonical layers: market metadata, fill-level trading records, and oracle-resolution events.

## Key Findings

- The dataset encompasses October 2020 through March 2026, containing over 770,000 markets, 943 million trades, and nearly 2 million oracle events
- Developed reproducible data infrastructure with incremental update mechanisms
- Demonstrated practical applications through two case studies: NBA outcome calibration and CPI expectation forecasting
- Successfully resolved data linkage challenges and implemented consistency mechanisms for ongoing synchronization
- Project available at polymonitor.club

Prior work shows that prediction-market prices can serve as high-frequency, continuously updated, and distributionally informative signals for studying macroeconomic expectations, electoral outcomes, and the financial effects of political and policy uncertainty.
