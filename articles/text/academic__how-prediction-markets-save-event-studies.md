# How Prediction Markets Can Save Event Studies

## Abstract

The paper examines how prediction market data can address methodological challenges in event studies. The researchers demonstrate that event study results are sensitive to three factors: selection of the event window, prior probability assignment at the window's start, and occurrence of confounding events. They propose that prediction markets can mitigate these biases in applications spanning political economy and related fields.

## Key Findings

- Prediction market data improves event study methodology by providing objective probability estimates for events, reducing researcher discretion
- Clear event window identification through market-based timing signals
- Control for confounding events by isolating the effect of specific news
- By combining prediction market data with the S&P 500, researchers can identify causal effects of electoral outcomes on financial variables
