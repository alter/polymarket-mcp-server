# Prediction Markets in Theory and Practice (Wolfers & Zitzewitz, 2006)

## Abstract

This paper examines prediction markets (also called "information markets," "idea futures," or "event futures"), which are trading venues where contract payoffs depend on future events. These markets generate prices that function as aggregated forecasts. The authors review theoretical work emphasizing efficient information aggregation and empirical evidence demonstrating that market-generated forecasts typically outperform most moderately sophisticated benchmarks.

## Key Topics Covered

- Theoretical contributions on information aggregation efficiency
- Empirical applications and performance comparisons
- Future research opportunities in the field
- Discussion of "Five Open Questions About Prediction Markets"
- Use of markets to inform policy
