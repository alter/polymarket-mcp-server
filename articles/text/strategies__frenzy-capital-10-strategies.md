# Trading Strategies for Prediction Markets: Ten Empirically-Grounded Approaches

**Source:** Frenzy Capital, April 2026. Synthesizes 20 academic studies.

## The Ten Core Strategies

### Strategy 1: Favourite-Longshot Bias
Low-probability contracts are systematically overpriced; high-probability ones are underpriced.
- **Approach:** Buy "No" on overpriced longshots; buy "Yes" on underpriced favourites
- **Edge:** 2–5%
- **Confidence:** "The single most robust finding in prediction market research"
- Persists across decades and markets

### Strategy 2: Hedge Underpricing
Market participants systematically undervalue hedging instruments by 40–50%.
- **Approach:** Purchase complementary contract pairs when combined price < $1.00
- **Returns:** 3–15%

### Strategy 3: Informed Trader Flow Signals
Late-stage trading near event resolution reflects superior information.
- **Approach:** Follow sharp price movements (5+ pp) in final hours with volume confirmation
- **Edge:** 2–5%

### Strategy 4: Multi-Platform Arbitrage
Price discrepancies across exchanges create risk-free opportunities.
- When combined YES/NO prices sum < $1.00 across platforms → guaranteed profit
- **Returns:** 1–5% per arbitrage
- ~$40 million extracted historically on Polymarket

### Strategy 5: Long-Horizon Forecasting Edge
Prediction markets outperform polls and experts at 100+ day horizons.
- **Advantage:** 5–15% forecast improvements
- Markets predicted replication failures 1–2 months early

### Strategy 6: Price Interpretation at Extremes
Contracts near $0 or $1 diverge from true probability due to risk-averse traders.
- Apply 3–8% corrections toward $0.50 for improved accuracy
- Useful defensively when pricing these extremes yourself

### Strategy 7: Political Connection Trading
Connected firms experience abnormal stock returns following prediction market probability shifts.
- Stock market lags prediction markets by 48–72 hours
- Enables lagging-indicator trade

### Strategy 8: Polymarket Intra-Market & Combinatorial Arbitrage
**Market Rebalancing:** When YES+NO deviates from $1.00
**Combinatorial Arbitrage:** Across dependent markets
- **Scale:** ~$40 million extracted historically
- Median profit-per-dollar: ~$0.60 on detected opportunities

### Strategy 9: Liquidity Provision as Underwriting (Kalshi)
Passive liquidity providers function as underwriters, not market makers.
- NFL season example: ~$29 million aggregate profits
- Significant weekly volatility; terminal directional exposure

### Strategy 10: Dynamic Hedging
Lock in profits from odds movement regardless of final outcome.
- Open position at perceived mispricing
- Close when prices correct

## Platform Differences Matter

- Kalshi CFTC regulation vs Polymarket orderbook model affects fee treatment and hedging
- On Kalshi: achieve same economic exposure by buying No, not shorting directly
- Fee structures critically impact break-even thresholds

## Edge Durability

- Favourite-longshot bias: **Very High** confidence (persists for decades)
- Arbitrage opportunities close most quickly as markets develop
- As markets mature and attract sophisticated participants, edges narrow

## Key Risk Factors

- Ambiguous resolution criteria
- Regulatory uncertainty
- Platform failures
- Thin liquidity in emerging markets
