# Polymarket: How to Avoid Bots and Find Real Smart Money

Based on Hubble's audit of **1.46 million Polymarket addresses**.

## The Bot Problem

Only **3.7% of high-frequency accounts generated 37.44% of trading volume** — meaning most visible activity reflects algorithmic trading, not genuine market consensus.

Metric: 95% of human users maintain participation below ~300 markets; algorithmic accounts exceed 500+ markets regularly.

---

## Three Core Identification Methods

### Method 1: Market Participation Filter

Real traders participate in fewer markets than bots:
- Human threshold: <300 markets
- Bot threshold: >500 markets

### Method 2: Focus Ratio Analysis (XY-Axis Method)

True traders exhibit linear relationships between markets and trade frequency.
**Focus Ratio** = total trades / markets participated = 2–10 for humans

Bots deviate sharply:
- **Deep-focus bots**: Few markets, extreme trade frequency (ratios reaching 39,394)
- **Broad-scatter bots**: Many markets, minimal activity per market

### Method 3: Holding Duration — The Golden Standard

"Algorithms can mimic trading frequency but struggle replicating human time exposure"

| Trader Type | Holding Duration |
|-------------|-----------------|
| HFT/MEV bots | <60 seconds |
| Grid/market-making bots | <10 minutes |
| Real active traders | >1 hour |
| Trend holders | >1 day |

**Rule:** Positions held under 10 minutes = noise trading, not predictive signals.

---

## Three Survival Rules for Copy Traders

1. **Volume ≠ Consensus**: High-volume markets often trap retail into providing liquidity against hedging algorithms

2. **"Generalists" Are Usually "Workers"**: Those trading hundreds of topics simultaneously likely run systematic strategies, not informed predictions

3. **Time Exposure Reveals Truth**: True smart money accepts sustained risk exposure; embrace signals from patient, long-duration holders

## Additional Filters for Genuine Smart Money

From across multiple analyses:
- **Sample size**: At least 50–100 closed trades minimum to see real pattern
- **Win rate + PNL combined**: Don't evaluate win rate alone
- **Category focus**: Specialists beat generalists in their domain
- **Position building**: Over days/weeks, not single trades
- **Exit behavior**: Does the wallet exit before resolution or hold to settlement?
