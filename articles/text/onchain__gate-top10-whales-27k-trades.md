# Inside Polymarket's Top 10 Whales: 27,000 Trades, Illusion of Smart Money, Survival Rules

PANews conducted an in-depth analysis of **27,000 trades** made by the top 10 profit-making whales on Polymarket in December to uncover the truth behind their profits.

## Key Finding #1: Hedging Is Far More Complex Than Advertised

While many so-called "smart money" traders do use hedging arbitrage, their actual strategies are far more complex than simple hedging described on social media. Rather than just pairing YES/NO positions, these traders leverage sports betting rules like "over/under" and "win/loss" to build intricate hedged portfolios.

Example — NBA game (76ers vs Mavericks): One whale simultaneously bought Under (small spread), Over (large spread), 76ers (home), Mavericks (away), and 11 other directions. He used an arbitrage strategy of insufficient probability — buying 76ers win probability at 56.8% and Mavericks at 39.37% (total cost ~0.962) — achieving a guaranteed profit state.

## Key Finding #2: The "Zombie Order" Problem — Win Rates Are Inflated

Behind remarkably high historical win rates lay a large number of unclosed "zombie orders," masking the true win rates, which were far lower than historical averages.

Example — **SeriousSirius**: apparent win rate vs. true win rate of only **53.3%**.

## Individual Whale Profiles

**DrPufferfish:**
- Profit-loss ratio: 8.62 (high profit expectation)
- Strategy: Professional prediction analysis + strict position management
- Most hedge trades were in loss state; total P&L of hedge orders: **-$2.09 million** (used as insurance)
- Strategy is NOT simple arbitrage hedge

**gmanas (3rd place):**
- Total profit December: **$1.97 million**
- True win rate: **51.8%**
- Completed over **2,400 predictions** → clearly automated execution
- Verdict: algorithmic strategy

**Non-sports whale (unique case):**
- Earned ~**$929,000** with almost no zombie orders (just $8,800 unrealized losses)

- Only top whale NOT focused on sports

**0x006cc (8th place):**
- Net profit: ~**$1.27 million**, true win rate: **54%**
- Only **0.7 trades per day** — clearly not automated
- Early: simple manual hedging; December: upgraded to complex hedging
- Illustrates rapid strategy evolution as more traders learn hedging

**Cavs2 (10th place):**
- Actual total profit: ~**$630,000**
- Real win rate: **~50.43%**
- Low risk-hedging ratio: 6.6%
- Prefers heavy betting on NHL hockey (one side)
- Luck plays significant role; not very instructive for copy traders

## Five Harsh Truths About "Smart Money"

1. Hedging arbitrage is prone to losses
2. Copy trading is ineffective
3. Managing profit-loss ratios is key (not win rate alone)
4. The real secrets lie beyond mathematical formulas
5. Profit scale in prediction markets is limited

## Key Insight for Copy Traders

A "Quant Hedge" whale may bet both YES and NO in the same market (delta-neutral strategy). Blindly copying one leg of their trade — without understanding the full picture — can result in losses even when the whale is profitable overall.

## The Bottom Line

In Polymarket's prediction market, most so-called "legendary whales" are either lucky survivors or relentless grinders. The real secrets to wealth aren't found in inflated win rate leaderboards, but in the algorithms of a select few top players who put real capital on the line after filtering out noise.
