# Polymarket 2025: Six Profit Models (86–95M Transaction Analysis)

Study of 86–95 million blockchain transactions on Polymarket, identifying six primary earning strategies. Key finding: only **0.51% of wallets** achieved profits exceeding $1,000.

---

## Strategy 1: Information Arbitrage

The premier edge — superior data gathering.

Example: A French trader ("Théo"/"Theo4") earned **$85 million** during the 2024 US presidential election by commissioning custom polling research asking "what do your neighbors support?" rather than direct preference questions.
- Investment: <$10,000 in surveys
- Return: $85 million
- Lesson: Superior information gathering, not prediction ability, drives the largest profits

---

## Strategy 2: Cross-Platform Arbitrage

Exploit price differences between Polymarket and Kalshi.

Documented example: "One trader simultaneously bought both YES and NO contracts across platforms for 93 cents, receiving $1 regardless of outcome — **7.5% risk-free profit in one hour**."

- Extracted value: $40+ million (April 2024 – April 2025)
- Risk: Differing settlement definitions between platforms can eliminate apparent arbitrage
- 2026 status: 73% of arbitrage profits captured by sub-100ms execution bots

---

## Strategy 3: High-Probability Bonds Strategy

Purchasing near-certain events (95%+ probability).

Example: Buying a contract at 0.95 for 72 hours before a rate decision, collecting $1 = **5% in 3 days** = 1,800% annualized.

Repeating twice weekly generates substantial compounding returns. Low risk, highly scalable, but requires identifying such events systematically.

---

## Strategy 4: Liquidity Provision / Market Making

Act as market maker — quote both buy and sell prices simultaneously, earn spreads.

Notable trader: **@defiance_cr** scaled from $10,000 to $700–800 daily profit through automated systems.

New markets yield **80–200% annualized returns** on liquidity provision. Requires automation.

---

## Strategy 5: Narrow Specialization (Domain Expertise)

Experts dominating specific niches maintain 60–96% win rates.

Notable trader: **HyperLiquid0xb** earned $1.4M+ specializing in sports markets through deep domain knowledge.

Successful specialists: baseball, political speech analysis, weather forecasting using public meteorological data (entering only when win probability exceeded 0.77).

---

## Strategy 6: Speed Trading / Temporal Arbitrage

Algorithms exploit information-to-price reaction delays (seconds to minutes).

- Approximately **10,200 rapid trades** generated $420,000 collectively in 2024–2025
- Advantage diminishes as institutional capital arrives
- 2026: This edge largely captured by professional firms (Susquehanna, Jump Trading)

---

## Risk Management of Successful Traders

- Maintain **5–12 uncorrelated positions** simultaneously
- **5–10% risk per trade**
- Portfolio diversification prevents single-event catastrophes
- **91% of top wallets** exit positions before final settlement (capturing avg 73% of potential profit)

---

## Key Findings from Million-Wallet Analysis

Research on 1.3M+ Polymarket wallets:
- Wallets with **100+ trades** and **win rate above 60%** considered skill-based
- 60–65% win rate = skill-based edge
- 65–70% = domain expertise
- >70% = exceptional (but may reflect small sample size)
- Look for **consistent monthly profits** across multiple markets, not single lucky windfall
