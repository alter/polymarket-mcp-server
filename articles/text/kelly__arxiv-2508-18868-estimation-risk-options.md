# Tackling Estimation Risk in Kelly Investing Using Options

**Authors:** Fabrizio Lillo, Piero Mazzarisi, Ioanna-Yvonni Tsaknaki  
**arXiv ID:** 2508.18868

## Abstract Summary

The Kelly criterion, introduced by Kelly (1956), revolutionized the fields of gambling and portfolio optimization by providing a robust framework for maximizing long-term wealth growth while controlling market risk. Despite its theoretical elegance, the Kelly criterion faces two main criticisms: large short-term risk and high sensitivity to estimation errors.

This paper proposes a solution to this problem for a binomial tree market by integrating option trading with log-optimal portfolios to mitigate estimation risk within the Kelly framework. A proper convex combination of Kelly with Options (KO) strategies is proved to be asymptotically robust to any parameter misspecification.

## Key Contributions

1. Formal identification of estimation risk as the primary practical challenge of Kelly investing
2. Novel construction: options combined with Kelly portfolios to hedge against parameter uncertainty
3. Proof that KO strategies are asymptotically robust to any parameter misspecification
4. Practical guidance for implementing Kelly under parameter uncertainty

## Relevance

Estimation risk — not knowing the true win probability — is the central challenge in prediction market Kelly sizing. This paper offers a formal mechanism (using options or hedging instruments) to remain Kelly-optimal despite this uncertainty.
