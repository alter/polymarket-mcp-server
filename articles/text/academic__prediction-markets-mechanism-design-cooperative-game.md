# Prediction Markets, Mechanism Design, and Cooperative Game Theory

## Abstract

The paper examines prediction markets through a mechanism design lens. Prediction markets are designed to elicit information from multiple agents in order to predict future events. The author develops mechanisms based on proper scoring rules while analyzing their incentive compatibility properties and connections to cooperative game theory.

## Key Findings

- The research bridges two traditionally separate fields by proposing a unified framework
- Conitzer's main contribution involves mechanisms that employ proper scoring rules to encourage truthful information revelation
- The work establishes links between prediction market designs and cooperative game theory concepts, including the Shapley value and VCG mechanisms
- Considers a type of direct-revelation prediction market, in which agents report their full information directly

Published at: Twenty-Fifth Conference on Uncertainty in Artificial Intelligence (UAI 2009).
