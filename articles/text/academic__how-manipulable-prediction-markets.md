# How Manipulable Are Prediction Markets?

## Abstract

This paper conducts a large-scale field experiment to investigate the manipulability of prediction markets. The main experiment involves randomly shocking prices across 817 separate markets, with hourly price data collected to examine whether effects of these shocks persist.

## Key Findings

- Prediction markets can be successfully manipulated: effects of trades are visible even 60 days after they have occurred
- Manipulation effects persist for extended periods but gradually diminish over time
- Markets with more traders, greater trading volume, and an external source of probability estimates are harder to manipulate
- Greater trading volume provides natural protection against price distortions
