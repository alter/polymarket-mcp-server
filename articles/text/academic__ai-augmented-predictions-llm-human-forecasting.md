# AI-Augmented Predictions: LLM Assistants Improve Human Forecasting Accuracy

## Abstract

The researchers investigated whether large language models could enhance human forecasting performance. They tested two LLM assistants — one providing high-quality advice and another intentionally designed to be overconfident — against a control group using a less advanced model. Participants (N=991) answered six forecasting questions with optional assistant access.

## Key Findings

- Both frontier LLM assistants significantly enhanced prediction accuracy by between 24 percent and 28 percent relative to the control group
- When excluding outlier data, the superforecasting assistant improved accuracy by 41%, versus 29% for the noisy assistant
- The research does not consistently support hypotheses that improvements disproportionately benefit less skilled forecasters or reduce prediction diversity
- Further research into the robustness of this pattern is needed, particularly regarding outlier effects
