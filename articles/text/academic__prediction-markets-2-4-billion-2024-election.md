# Prediction Markets? The Accuracy and Efficiency of $2.4 Billion in the 2024 Presidential Election

## Abstract

The researchers examined over 2,500 political prediction markets across four platforms during the final five weeks of the 2024 U.S. presidential campaign, analyzing more than $2 billion in transactions to determine whether market prices accurately and efficiently aggregate political information. Platforms analyzed: Iowa Electronic Markets, Kalshi, PredictIt, and Polymarket.

## Key Findings

- Accuracy varied significantly by platform: PredictIt markets achieved 93% accuracy, while Kalshi reached 78% and Polymarket only 67%
- Markets failed to demonstrate efficiency, with identical contracts showing divergent prices across exchanges
- Daily price movements displayed weak or negative autocorrelation, suggesting inefficient price discovery
- Arbitrage opportunities were most pronounced in the final two weeks before Election Day

The research challenges assumptions that political prediction markets necessarily aggregate information accurately or efficiently.
