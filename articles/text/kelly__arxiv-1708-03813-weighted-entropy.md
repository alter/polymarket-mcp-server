# Weighted Entropy and Optimal Portfolios for Risk-Averse Kelly Investments

**Authors:** Mark Kelbert, Izabella Stuhl, Yuri Suhov  
**arXiv ID:** 1708.03813 (math.PR)  
**Submitted:** August 12, 2017

## Abstract Overview

The paper examines log-optimal investment strategies that incorporate weighted evaluations of different outcomes. The researchers present two primary findings:

1. Logarithmic growth rates can produce supermartingales under specific conditions
2. Optimal investment strategies follow proportional betting patterns

The work states: "The optimal (martingale) investment strategy is a proportional betting" and notes that "the investment does not exceed the current capital value and allows the trader to cover the worst possible losses."

## Key Focus Areas

- Extension of capital growth investment theory
- Weighted entropy applications to portfolio optimization
- Discrete-time mathematical models
- Risk-averse modifications to the classical Kelly betting scheme
- Practical constraints ensuring traders can manage losses

## Subject Classification

Mathematics > Probability (math.PR)

## Relevance

Bridges information theory (weighted entropy) with financial portfolio management, offering theoretical foundations for conservative (risk-averse) investment strategies that modify classical Kelly by weighting outcomes differently.
