# Semantic Trading: Agentic AI for Clustering and Relationship Discovery in Prediction Markets

## Abstract

Prediction markets allow users to trade on outcomes of real-world events, but are prone to fragmentation through overlapping questions and hidden contradictions. This paper presents an agentic AI pipeline that autonomously clusters markets into coherent topical groups and identifies within-cluster market pairs whose resolved outcomes exhibit strong dependence.

## Key Findings

- The agent-identified relationships achieved roughly 60-70% accuracy in predicting market relationships
- Trading strategies based on discovered relationships generated about 20% average returns over week-long horizons
- The approach demonstrates that agentic AI and large language models can uncover hidden semantic patterns in prediction markets
- The research was evaluated using historical data from Polymarket
