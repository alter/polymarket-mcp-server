# Six Profitable Trading Models on Polymarket (Based on On-Chain Analysis)

Based on analysis of **86+ million on-chain transactions**, researchers identified six verified strategies:

## 1. Information Arbitrage

A French trader named Théo leveraged custom polling data ("neighbor effect") to predict the 2024 election, netting $85 million. The strategy involves "asking the right questions" rather than possessing superior predictive ability, then positioning accordingly when markets misprice based on incomplete information.

**Key:** Commission unique data sources that the market does not have. Custom polls, proprietary surveys, expert networks.

## 2. Cross-Platform Arbitrage

Traders exploit price discrepancies across prediction market platforms.

**Example:** Buying YES at $0.45 on Polymarket while selling NO at $0.48 on a competitor → near-certain profit.

**Critical challenge:** Matching settlement definitions precisely — subtle differences can eliminate profits. The 2024 US government shutdown case: Polymarket judged "shutdown occurred" (YES), Kalshi judged "shutdown did not occur" (NO).

**Scale:** Over $40 million in risk-free profits extracted.

## 3. High-Probability "Bond" Strategy

Rather than seeking excitement, sophisticated traders purchase contracts trading above $0.95 for nearly-certain outcomes.

- A single trade yielding 5% might seem modest
- Executing two trades weekly → annualized returns exceeding 1800% through compounding
- **Risk:** "Pseudo-certainty" events hiding black swan risks

Increasingly dominated by institutional algorithmic traders.

## 4. Liquidity Provision (Market Making)

Market makers simultaneously place buy and sell orders, profiting from spreads.

- One practitioner earned $700–800 daily through automated systems
- Exploits new market inefficiencies and reward programs
- Polymarket pays 20–25% of taker fees to makers as rebates

**Limitation:** Institutional competition and declining incentives reducing returns.

## 5. Domain Specialization

Top earners focus narrowly on single domains.

**Example:** One trader maintained 96% accuracy predicting speech content by analyzing historical statements.

Specialization requires substantial time investment but creates sustainable competitive advantages difficult to replicate.

## 6. Speed Trading (High-Frequency)

Sophisticated traders capitalize on information-to-market-price delays (measured in seconds).

- Algorithmic systems monitoring live events establish positions before broader market digestion
- Between 2024–2025, top algorithmic traders executed 10,200+ high-speed trades generating $4.2M profit
- Tools: low-latency API access, real-time news monitoring, pre-defined decision scripts, distributed capital

**Note:** This advantage erodes as institutional participation increases.

## Key Statistics

- **0.51%** of traders achieve significant profits through systematic approaches
- **7.6%** of wallets are profitable overall
- Profitable traders primarily systematic, not intuitive
