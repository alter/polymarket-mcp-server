# Closing Line Value (CLV) Demystified

**Author:** Joseph Buchdahl (Football-Data.co.uk, Tennis-Data.co.uk, 60+ Pinnacle articles)

## What is the Closing Line?

The final odds offered by a sportsbook just before a betting market closes, immediately before an event begins. Adjusted by:
1. **News**: team/player/event information
2. **Money**: customer betting flows

Pinnacle is recognized as "the most accurate sportsbook" due to its sharp-bettor-friendly model (no restrictions on winners).

## What is Closing Line Value?

CLV = (odds at bet placement) / (closing odds)

**Formula:** If true odds were 2.00 but offered at 2.10, CLV = 2.10/2.00 = 1.05 (5% edge)

Assumption: closing odds represent fair outcome probabilities after removing vig.

## Why CLV Matters: Statistical Efficiency

Standard deviation comparison:
- Even-money profit/loss: SD ≈ **1.00**
- CLV: SD ≈ **0.10**

Therefore: **~50 bets** may demonstrate statistical significance for CLV, vs. **thousands** needed for profit/loss records.

This means CLV "can quickly tell you when CLV may have disappeared, and whether it might be time to adjust or change forecasting strategy."

## Empirical Validation

Buchdahl's Wisdom of the Crowd system (~20,000 bets):
- Actual profit: **3.4%** over turnover
- Expected value (CLV-implied): **4.0%**
- Results within statistical significance bounds

## De-Vigging Methods

To extract fair closing probabilities from Pinnacle odds:
1. Equal margin method (crude)
2. Odds ratio method
3. Logarithm function
4. **Shin method** (accounts for favourite-longshot bias — recommended)

## Pinnacle Odds Dropper Evidence

Analysis of 952 positive expected value bets across football markets:
- **756 bets (79%)** experienced price shortening at competing books
- Average price shortening: **3.94%**
- Statistical testing: "effectively impossible these price movements happened by chance"

## Two Bettor Archetypes

**Odds Originators:** Sharp bettors with novel forecasting models — their information hasn't yet moved market pricing. May not initially show CLV as they ARE the market.

**Line Shoppers:** Compare Pinnacle (sharp) vs. recreational books (slow). The gap = consistent positive CLV opportunity.

## Transferable Edge for Polymarket

1. Track your entry price vs. the price at market resolution (closing equivalent)
2. Consistently beating the close = process-level validation of model edge
3. CLV is a leading indicator of profitability — ~50 trades to know if your edge is real
4. Use Pinnacle de-vigged odds as benchmark for true probability estimates
