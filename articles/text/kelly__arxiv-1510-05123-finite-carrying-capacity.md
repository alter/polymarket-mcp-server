# Optimal Growth Trajectories with Finite Carrying Capacity

**Authors:** Francesco Caravelli, Lorenzo Sindoni, Fabio Caccioli, Cozmin Ududec  
**arXiv ID:** 1510.05123  
**Submitted:** October 17, 2015 (last revised June 20, 2016)  
**Published:** Physical Review E 94, 022315 (2016)

## Abstract Summary

The paper addresses optimal strategy development for maximizing average growth rates in multiplicative stochastic processes. While the Kelly criterion solves this for geometric Brownian motion through constant resource allocation, the authors extend the analysis to systems featuring finite carrying capacity constraints.

The researchers formulate the problem using "a stochastic process with multiplicative noise and a non-linear drift term" shaped by carrying capacity functions. They analyze two functional categories — power laws and logarithmic functions — deriving optimal control parameter trajectories. Numerical simulations validate their analytical findings.

## Research Areas

- Primary: Portfolio Management (q-fin.PM)
- Secondary: Trading and Market Microstructure (q-fin.TR)

## Applications

The work bridges quantitative finance with biological and ecological modeling, relevant to systems where growth constraints become operative over time. In prediction market terms: applicable when position size is bounded by available liquidity.
