# Information Aggregation with AI Agents

## Abstract

The research investigates whether large language models can effectively aggregate dispersed private information through trading in prediction markets. The study measures information aggregation using log error of final prices.

## Key Findings

- AI agents show effective information aggregation in simple scenarios but struggle significantly with complex information structures
- The agents demonstrate cognitive limitations comparable to humans when reasoning about others' knowledge
- Information aggregation remains robust and unaffected by allowing cheap talk communication, changing the duration of the market or initial price, and strategic prompting
- More advanced AI agents perform better and achieve higher profits
- Counterintuitively, providing feedback about past performance had no measurable impact

The research suggests prediction markets function reliably even as complexity increases, though AI agent performance declines substantially with more intricate information structures.
