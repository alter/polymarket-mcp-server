# Price Discovery and Trading in Modern Prediction Markets

**Authors:** Hunter Ng, Lin Peng, Yubo Tao, Dexin Zhou
**Source:** SSRN Working Paper (abstractid=5331995)

## Abstract

Provides the first evidence on price discovery dynamics across modern prediction markets, examining a unique dataset of common contracts traded on Polymarket, Kalshi, PredictIt, and Robinhood during the period leading up to the 2024 U.S. presidential election.

## Key Findings

1. **Liquidity advantage:** More liquid prediction markets substantially outperform polls in predicting election results.

2. **Price disparities:** Significant price disparities exist across platforms — Polymarket was found to lead Kalshi in price discovery, particularly when liquidity and trading activity are high.

3. **Arbitrage opportunities:** The lead-lag relationship between platforms implies economically meaningful arbitrage opportunities.

4. **Platform ranking:** Polymarket → Kalshi → PredictIt → Robinhood in terms of price leadership.

## Relevance to Prediction-Market Pricing

Establishes Polymarket as the price-leading platform in the US prediction market ecosystem. For cross-platform strategies: Polymarket prices should be treated as leading indicators of other platforms' eventual price moves.
