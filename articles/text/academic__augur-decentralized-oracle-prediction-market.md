# Augur: A Decentralized Oracle and Prediction Market Platform

## Abstract

The paper describes Augur as "a trustless, decentralized oracle and platform for prediction markets." Market outcomes are determined by users holding the Reputation token, who stake tokens on actual results and receive settlement fees. The system incentivizes honest reporting through economic mechanisms where token holders can dispute outcomes by posting escalating bonds.

## Key Findings

- The platform employs a novel dispute resolution mechanism where sufficiently large bond challenges cause Reputation to split into multiple versions corresponding to each possible outcome
- Participants must exchange their tokens for one version — since only the version reflecting reality maintains value, rational actors naturally gravitate toward the accurate version
- This creates a self-reinforcing truthfulness incentive without requiring a trusted central authority

Submitted January 5, 2015; Latest revision: November 30, 2020. The first major academic paper on decentralized prediction market design.
