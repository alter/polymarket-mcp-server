# Political Shocks and Price Discovery in Prediction Markets

## Abstract

Using transaction-level trade data from Polymarket's 2024 U.S. presidential election market, the authors study how prediction markets process shocks, analyzing three events: the Biden-Trump debate, the assassination attempt on Trump, and Biden's dropout.

## Key Findings

- Trading rises after each shock, especially among incumbent traders with pre-event exposure against a Trump victory, who are also more likely to flip positions
- **Debate impact:** Price movements from the debate largely reversed afterward, suggesting temporary market adjustment
- **Assassination attempt:** Repricing from this event persisted over time, indicating sustained market reassessment
- **Biden's dropout:** Generated two-sided trading activity with minimal net price movement, reflecting genuine disagreement among traders
- Post-shock price dynamics correlate with market liquidity and trader disagreement about how political events translate into election probability changes
