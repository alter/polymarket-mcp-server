# Price Discovery and Trading in Modern Prediction Markets

## Abstract

This study provides the first evidence on price discovery dynamics across modern prediction markets, using a unique dataset of common contracts traded on leading platforms — Polymarket, Kalshi, PredictIt, and Robinhood — during the period leading up to the 2024 U.S. presidential election.

## Key Findings

- More liquid prediction markets substantially outperform polls in predicting subsequent election results, yet there are significant price disparities across platforms
- Polymarket leads Kalshi in price discovery, particularly when liquidity and trading activity are high, implying economically meaningful arbitrage opportunities
- Net order imbalance from large trades strongly predicts subsequent returns
- The market experiencing greater directional order flow from large trades tends to lead price discovery
- Platform structure, liquidity, and informed trading interact to shape trading and prices
