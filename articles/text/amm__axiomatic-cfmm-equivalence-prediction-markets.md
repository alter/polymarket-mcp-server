# An Axiomatic Characterization of CFMMs and Equivalence to Prediction Markets

**Authors:** Rafael Frongillo, Maneesha Papireddygari, Bo Waggoner

**arXiv ID:** 2302.00196 | Published at ITCS 2024

## Abstract

Constant-function market makers (CFMMs), such as Uniswap, are automated exchanges offering trades among a set of assets. This paper studies their technical relationship to another class of automated market makers: cost-function prediction markets. The authors introduce axioms for market makers and show that CFMMs with concave potential functions characterize "good" market makers according to these axioms. They then show that every such CFMM on n assets is equivalent to a cost-function prediction market for events with n outcomes, and their construction directly converts a CFMM into a prediction market and vice versa.

## Key Contributions

- **Axiomatic Framework:** Introduces axioms characterizing effective market makers, showing CFMMs with concave potential functions satisfy these criteria
- **Equivalence Result:** Proves every such CFMM on n assets is mathematically equivalent to a cost-function prediction market for n-outcome events, with direct conversion methods
- **Conceptual Insight:** "Markets are good at facilitating trade if and only if they are good at revealing beliefs" — linking market-making axioms to information-elicitation principles
- **Practical Applications:** Demonstrates how liquidity strategies and tools transfer between prediction markets and CFMMs, yielding novel market designs
- **Scoring Rules:** Shows each CFMM implicitly defines a proper scoring rule for belief elicitation; the scoring rule for Uniswap is unusual but known

## Technical Details

The equivalence is established via convex duality: the correspondence goes to the scoring rule generated by G = C* (the convex conjugate of C), and vice versa. Keywords: Convex analysis, Equivalence result, Axiomatic characterization, Market Makers, Prediction markets, Scoring rules, Cost-functions.
