# SoK: Market Microstructure for Decentralized Prediction Markets (DePMs)

**Authors:** Nahid Rahman, Joseph Al-Chami, Jeremy Clark

**Submitted:** October 17, 2025; Last revised March 13, 2026

**arXiv:** 2510.15612

## Abstract

This Systematization of Knowledge (SoK) paper surveys decentralized prediction markets dating back to 2011. Modern DePMs like Polymarket deviate materially from earlier designs like Truthcoin and Augur v1.

The authors introduce a modular eight-stage workflow:
1. Underlying infrastructure
2. Market topic
3. Share structure and pricing
4. Market initialization
5. Trading
6. Market resolution
7. Settlement
8. Archiving

## Key Findings

- Hundreds of DePM proposals exist with varying architectural approaches
- Practical divergence between theoretical early designs and contemporary CLOB implementations
- Design variants present substantive trade-offs across decentralization, expressiveness, and manipulation resistance
- Open research problems identified across all eight workflow stages

## Relevance to Polymarket CLOB Trading

- Comprehensive taxonomy of DePM designs including Polymarket's hybrid-decentralized CLOB
- Settlement and resolution mechanisms directly affect spread behavior near expiry
- Archiving and latency effects documented
- Manipulation resistance mechanisms relevant to order flow toxicity analysis
