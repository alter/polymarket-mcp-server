# Prediction Laundering: The Illusion of Neutrality, Transparency, and Governance in Polymarket

## Abstract

The researchers conduct a sociotechnical audit of Polymarket to examine how probabilistic authority is created. They introduce "Prediction Laundering" — a process where subjective bets and capital-driven activity are obscured through algorithmic aggregation, presenting them as objective truth.

## Key Findings

1. **Four-Stage Laundering Cycle:** The authors identify Structural Sanitization, Probabilistic Flattening, Architectural Masking, and Epistemic Hardening as stages that strip away original uncertainty from predictions.

2. **Epistemic Stratification:** Technical experts understand underlying mechanisms while the broader public receives a "sanitized, capital-weighted signal," creating knowledge asymmetries.

3. **Governance Gaps:** The research reveals how "truth-resolution" shifts to off-platform communities like Discord, creating accountability voids.

4. **Methodological Approach:** The study employs digital ethnography, interpretive walkthroughs, and 27 semi-structured interviews to examine market dynamics.

The authors advocate for "Friction-Positive Design" that makes visible the social and financial complexities inherent in prediction market operations.
