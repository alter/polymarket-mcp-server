# Fed Rate Cut Prediction Markets: Trading Guide

## Market Mechanics

Kalshi offers binary contracts on Federal Reserve decisions. Common structures include whether the Fed will cut rates at specific meetings, predictions about future rate levels, and contracts counting total cuts in a given year. "Prices reflect implied probabilities. If 'Fed cuts in June' is trading at 65¢, the market implies a 65% chance of a cut at that meeting."

## Reading the Odds

Market prices directly translate to probabilities:
- 80¢ → strong consensus for a cut
- 50¢ → genuine uncertainty
- 20¢ → cut is unlikely

These signals can be compared against CME FedWatch data, which may reveal trading opportunities when the two platforms diverge.

## Key Market Drivers

- **Economic data**: CPI, PCE, and jobs reports are the primary movers
- **Fed communications**: Speeches from leadership and policy signals shift expectations
- **Growth indicators**: GDP data affects urgency to adjust rates
- **FOMC materials**: Minutes and dot plot releases reveal internal perspectives
- **External shocks**: Global events and trade policy can force Fed responses

## Trading Strategies

1. Position before major economic releases based on expectations versus consensus
2. Capitalize on market overreactions to single data points
3. Trade different meeting dates to express views on policy timing
4. Pair Fed market positions with related markets like stocks or crypto
5. Exit profitable positions before meetings rather than holding through announcements

## Common Pitfalls

- Entering positions after news breaks (edge already gone)
- Ignoring consensus expectations
- Overconcentrating in single meetings
- Misinterpreting probability as certainty
- Overlooking the Fed chair's press conference tone shifts

## Arbitrage Opportunities

CME FedWatch uses Fed funds futures while Kalshi uses direct binary contracts. They usually agree closely but can diverge during volatile periods — particularly around speeches and data releases — creating cross-venue trading opportunities.
