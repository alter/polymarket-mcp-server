# Beating the Best Constant Rebalancing Portfolio: A Generalization of the Kelly Criterion and Universal Learning Algorithm for Markets with Serial Dependence

**Author:** Duy Khanh Lam  
**arXiv ID:** 2507.05994  
**Submitted:** July 8, 2025

## Abstract Summary

The paper proposes a learning algorithm that leverages inherent serial dependence in asset returns to develop investment strategies that exceed the cumulative wealth of optimal constant rebalancing portfolios, addressing a significant gap in existing online portfolio optimization methods.

## Core Innovation

The research generalizes the classical Kelly criterion — traditionally requiring independent asset returns — to accommodate serial dependence patterns. As stated in the abstract: "the classical Kelly criterion, which requires independent assets' returns, is generalized to accommodate serial dependence in a market modeled as an independent and identically distributed process of random matrices."

## Technical Achievement

The algorithm learns temporal dependencies gradually from revealed market data without distributional assumptions, enabling superior performance in markets exhibiting calendar effects and other patterns.

## Validation

Experimental results using real market data confirm the theoretical guarantees and demonstrate practical effectiveness when serial dependence is significant.

## Relevance for Prediction Markets

Prediction markets often exhibit serial dependence: the same traders, the same information environment, repeated market structures. This generalization of Kelly to serial-dependent markets could enable better position sizing when accounting for temporal correlations in prediction market returns.
