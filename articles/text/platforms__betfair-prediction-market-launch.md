# Betfair Eyes Prediction Market Growth with 'Betfair Predicts'

## Overview
Flutter-owned Betfair added a new layer to its Exchange product called **'Betfair Predicts'**, offering prediction-market-style Yes/No choices layered on existing exchange liquidity.

## Product Details
- Not a standalone product — uses existing Exchange liquidity
- Simplified UI: Yes or No choice on a given outcome, followed by stake
- Currently testing with small, select group of account holders on invite-only basis
- Covers politics, sport, and entertainment outcomes

## Market Structure Findings
While betting exchanges generally offer higher odds/returns than bookmakers in **liquid markets**, the opposite is true in **illiquid markets** — this rationalized Betfair's decision to start offering quoted odds in addition to exchange-based odds (since February 2013).

This mirrors the shift in financial market structures from pure quote-driven or pure order-driven to **hybrid structures** combining advantages of both.

## Competitive Position
For prediction markets on politics, crypto, or non-sport events, "Betfair can't compete with Polymarket." Polymarket: more categories, lower fees, deeper liquidity.

Betfair still useful for: cricket, Premier League football, horse racing (strong liquidity). However, **Expert Fee** has made it hostile territory for profitable traders — consistent winners face an increasingly large cut.

## Betfair as Exchange vs. Prediction Market
Betfair is the world's oldest and largest betting exchange (since 2000). Betting exchanges are fundamentally prediction markets — the "Price Beacons" feature and exchange mechanics are structurally identical to prediction market CLOBs.

Projected US prediction market size: $1 trillion annually by 2030 (Eilers & Krejcik Gaming).
