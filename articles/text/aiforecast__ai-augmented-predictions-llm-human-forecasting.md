# AI-Augmented Predictions: LLM Assistants Improve Human Forecasting Accuracy

**Authors:** Philipp Schoenegger, Peter S. Park, Ezra Karger, Sean Trott, Philip E. Tetlock

**arXiv:** 2402.07862 | Submitted: February 2024

## Abstract

Large language models (LLMs) match and sometimes exceeding human performance in many domains. This study explores the potential of LLMs to augment human judgement in a forecasting task.

## Key Methods

- 991 participants answered 6 forecasting questions
- Three assistant conditions:
  1. Superforecasting-style LLM (GPT-4-Turbo, high-quality advice)
  2. Noisy LLM (intentionally overconfident, neglects base rates)
  3. Control (less advanced model, no numerical predictions)
- Randomized controlled design

## Key Results

- Both frontier LLM assistants significantly improved prediction accuracy by 24-28% vs. control
- Excluding outlier question: superforecasting assistant +41%, noisy assistant +29%
- Positive effects even from intentionally noisy/overconfident LLM
- No evidence of disproportionate benefit for weaker forecasters
- No significant reduction in prediction diversity

## Relevance to Polymarket Trading

Even an imperfect LLM assistant significantly improves forecasting accuracy (+24-41%). The "noisy" LLM still helps — implying the threshold for "good enough" LLM assistance is low. Human+LLM outperforms human alone, which informs hybrid human+AI trading systems.
