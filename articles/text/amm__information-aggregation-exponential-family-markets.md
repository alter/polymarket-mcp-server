# Information Aggregation in Exponential Family Markets

**Authors:** Jacob Abernethy, Sindhu Kutty, Sebastien Lahaie, Rahul Sami

**arXiv ID:** 1402.5458

## Abstract

The researchers examine prediction market mechanisms called automated market makers through the lens of exponential family distributions. They establish connections between how markets aggregate information through pricing and how learning agents aggregate beliefs using these probability distributions. The work analyzes market behavior and price equilibrium when traders demonstrate risk aversion via exponential utility functions, and extends the analysis to alternative scenarios including budget-constrained traders. The paper adapts the generalized log scoring rule to a market scoring rule which leads to markets with simple closed-form cost functions.

## Key Contributions

- Designing AMM mechanisms using exponential family distributions as theoretical foundation
- Bridges information aggregation in markets with belief aggregation in statistical learning
- Analyzing market equilibrium under exponential utility assumptions and other trader models
- Establishes conditions for convergence to rational expectations equilibrium
- Provides closed-form cost functions derived from generalized log scoring rules
