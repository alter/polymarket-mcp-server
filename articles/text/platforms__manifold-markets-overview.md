# Manifold Markets — Review 2026

## What Is Manifold Markets?
Play-money prediction platform using "Mana" (virtual currency). Part forecasting site, part social platform, part user-generated market system. Real-money "sweepcash" introduced September 2024 but sunsetted March 28, 2025.

## How Trading Works
Mix of limit orders and AMM (automated market maker):
- Trades fill against open orders first, then against AMM
- Users buy "shares" at price determined by current bet
- Shares resolve to M$1 if correct, M$0 if wrong
- Probability converges as more trades occur

## Market Types
- **Binary**: Simple Yes/No
- **Multiple Choice**: Several competing outcomes
- **Numeric**: Forecasting specific number in range
- **Pseudo-Numeric**: Multiple-choice "buckets" for imprecise numeric resolution

## Forecasting Accuracy
EA Forum analysis: Manifold generally performed worse than Metaculus and real-money markets. Key reasons:
1. **"Yes Bias"**: Users prefer betting Yes on long-shots (more fun)
2. **Play-money noise**: Reduced financial discipline
3. Brier scores lag behind platforms with real financial stakes

## Converting Edge Into Returns
Manifold **cannot convert forecasting edge into financial returns**. For anyone with genuine forecasting edge, Polymarket is the only platform that converts advantage into withdrawable returns.

## Best Use Cases
- Practice forecasting
- Follow niche questions
- Build research tools
- Use market odds as community signal
- API/data access for research and bot workflows

## API & Research Utility
Full API + WebSocket feed + bulk data access. Used for:
- Embedding prediction market data in analytics dashboards
- Decision-making tools
- Research on play-money prediction market effectiveness

## Key Finding
There is substantial evidence that play-money prediction markets provide real forecasting power (examples: sports betting, internal prediction markets at Google). But for monetizing a real forecasting edge, Manifold is limited.
