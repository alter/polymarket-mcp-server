# Opinion Dynamics Explain Price Formation in Prediction Markets

## Abstract

The researchers propose an agent-based model where participants in prediction markets form opinions about event probabilities through social network interactions using the Deffuant model of opinion dynamics. The study shows this approach replicates the empirical properties of historical prediction market time series, including volatility clustering and fat-tailed distribution of returns.

## Key Findings

1. **Optimal Parameters:** The model performs best with convergence parameter μ=0.1 and confidence threshold ε=0.32, producing synthetic time series that closely match historical PredictIt market data.

2. **Opinion Clustering:** Results suggest prediction markets are best reproduced when opinions are scattered around a single opinion cluster, indicating participants reach near-consensus without perfect alignment.

3. **Robustness:** Even under worst-case parameter combinations, the model qualitatively reproduces stylized facts like volatility clustering and non-autocorrelated returns.

4. **Network Independence:** Network topology (complete graph, scale-free, random) minimally affects results, suggesting price formation is fundamentally driven by opinion dynamics rather than network structure.
