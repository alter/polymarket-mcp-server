# Metaculus Forecasting Strategy & Superforecaster Calibration

## What is Metaculus?
Online forecasting platform using time-weighted logarithmic scoring rule (proper scoring rule, rewards predictions aligned with true beliefs). Partnered with Good Judgment Inc. since 2022.

## Who Are Superforecasters?
Good Judgment Project (IARPA 2011–2015) identified top 0.2% of forecasters ("superforecasters"):
- Calibration: average difference between stated probability and true frequency = 0.01
- ~70% maintained superforecaster status from one year to the next
- Performance correlation between years: 0.65

**Key insight**: Accuracy scores *decrease* when you round superforecasters' predictions to nearest 0.1 — they use meaningful precision.

## Core Forecasting Strategies (Tetlock's "10 Commandments")
1. **Triage**: Reference predictions from others (FiveThirtyEight, Polymarket, Metaculus)
2. **Break**: Decompose intractable problems into tractable sub-problems (Fermi estimation)
3. **Balance inside and outside views**
4. **Balance under- and overreacting to evidence**
5. **Look for clashing causal forces**
6. **Extrapolate current trends linearly**
7. **Distinguish degrees of doubt** (as the problem permits)
8. **Balance under- and overconfidence**
9. **Look for errors behind your mistakes** (avoid hindsight bias)

## Crowd Wisdom
- Going from 8 to 128 predictors decreases Brier score by ~0.048 (~25% improvement)
- Teams of superforecasters outperformed individual superforecasters by ~23%
- Superforecasters make many small updates and occasional big updates (Bayesian)

## Calibration Pitfalls
- Overconfidence is common; found in 2016 analysis of Metaculus predictions
- Very well-calibrated for <1-year horizons; calibration degrades at longer horizons
- Things don't happen as often as predictors imagine

## AI vs. Superforecasters
State-of-the-art AI models still fall short of Metaculus superforecasters. Frontier models may be as good as the *average* human on forecasting sites. Estimated timeline for bots reaching superforecaster level: mid-2026 (median), 80% CI: Oct 2025 to 2030.

## Manifold Markets Context
Manifold generally performed worse than Metaculus and real-money markets in EA Forum analysis. Cause: "Yes Bias" (users prefer betting Yes on long-shots because it's more fun) + play-money reduces discipline. Useful as a forecasting training ground and research tool, not as a cash market.
