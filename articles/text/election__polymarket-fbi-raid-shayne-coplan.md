# FBI Raids Polymarket CEO Shayne Coplan's Home in Criminal Probe

**Source:** Multiple (ABC News, NBC News, CNBC, Fast Company, Newsweek)  
**Date:** November 13–14, 2024

## The Raid

The FBI seized a cellphone and other electronic devices belonging to Polymarket's CEO, Shayne Coplan, in a raid on his SoHo, New York City apartment early on November 13, 2024. FBI agents arrived around 6 a.m. with a warrant. Coplan (age 26) was home when numerous agents entered his apartment and turned over his devices to authorities. He was not arrested or charged.

## The Investigation

The New York Times reported the raid was part of a probe by the FBI and the U.S. Attorney's Office for the Southern District of New York into whether Polymarket was run as an unlicensed commodities exchange that allowed users in the United States to illegally place bets on its platform.

The investigation involved whether Polymarket violated a prior settlement with the U.S. government by allowing American-based users access to its platform. The 2022 settlement with the Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC) required Polymarket to pay a **$1.4 million penalty** for operating an illegal, unregistered "event markets" platform.

## Context: Election Betting and Trump's Victory

Polymarket brought in more than $3.6 billion from bets placed on the presidential election, including $1.5 billion on Trump and $1 billion on Vice President Kamala Harris. Reports indicated Polymarket's pro-Trump outlook was driven by a small number of "whales" including the French trader who bet approximately $30 million on a Trump victory.

## Coplan's Response

Coplan said on X: "It's discouraging that the current administration would seek a last-ditch effort to go after companies they deem to be associated with political opponents."

A Polymarket spokesperson called the raid "obvious political retribution by the outgoing administration against Polymarket for providing a market that correctly called the 2024 presidential election."

Coplan quipped "New phone, who dis?" on social media after his phone was confiscated.

## Aftermath

- No charges were ever filed against Coplan
- In **July 2025**, the U.S. Department of Justice and CFTC **dropped their investigations** into Polymarket without further action
- In **September 2025**, Polymarket acquired a CFTC-registered exchange and clearinghouse to secure a US operating license
- The company moved to legally resume service to US customers by end of 2025
