# Prediction Markets in the 2024 Presidential Election

**Source:** Ballotpedia  
**Date:** November 2024

## Overview

Reference article documenting the role of prediction markets in the 2024 U.S. presidential election, covering the major platforms, key statistics, and the intersection with polling.

## Key Platforms

**Polymarket:**
- Blockchain-based, USDC on Polygon network
- Not available to US users under ToS (accessed via VPNs)
- $3.3–3.7 billion wagered on presidential election
- Monthly active users: 13,000 (May 2024) → 220,000 (October 2024)

**Kalshi:**
- CFTC-regulated, based in US
- Won legal battle with CFTC in October 2024 allowing election contracts
- First legal US election futures in over 100 years
- Over 500 million site visits during election cycle

**PredictIt:**
- US-regulated, CFTC no-action exemption
- $850 per-contract cap (raised to $3,500 after 2025 CFTC settlement)
- 5,000 participant cap per market

## Timeline of Key Events

**June 27, 2024:** Biden-Trump debate — Polymarket Biden withdrawal odds surge from 20% to 70%

**July 2024:** Nate Silver joins Polymarket as advisor; monthly volume doubles from $111M to $213M

**October 2024 (mid):** French whale's accounts driving Trump odds to 60%; WSJ reports potential manipulation; Polymarket investigates

**October 24, 2024:** Polymarket identifies French whale as single trader, no manipulation found

**November 5, 2024:** Election day — prediction markets at 95%+ for Trump before midnight

**November 6, 2024:** Trump declared winner by AP; prediction markets vindicated

**November 13, 2024:** FBI raids Polymarket CEO Shayne Coplan's apartment

**November 2025:** Columbia study finds ~25% of Polymarket volume may be wash trades

## Statistical Accuracy (2024)

- Polymarket: 67% accuracy (Vanderbilt study of final 5 weeks)
- Kalshi: 78% accuracy
- PredictIt: 93% accuracy
- All three correctly identified presidential winner
- Significant divergence in down-ballot accuracy

## Cryptocurrency-Partisan Correlation

Approximately 39.3% of all Polymarket traders participated exclusively in Trump YES market vs. 18.4% in Harris-exclusive markets, reflecting the platform's libertarian/crypto-native user base that skewed heavily pro-Trump.
