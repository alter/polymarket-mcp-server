# Decentralized Prediction Markets and Sports Books

## Abstract

This work considers a decentralized framework for prediction markets using automated market makers (AMMs), constructing a liquidity-based AMM structure that, under reasonable axioms on the underlying utility function, satisfies meaningful financial properties on the cost of betting and the resulting pricing oracle.

## Key Findings

- The paper constructs an AMM structure for prediction markets covering weather, politics, sports, and economics forecasting
- Studies how liquidity can be pooled or withdrawn from the AMM and the resulting implications to market behavior
- Proposes financially meaningful fees that can be collected for trading to compensate liquidity providers
- The framework satisfies important financial criteria on betting costs and pricing
