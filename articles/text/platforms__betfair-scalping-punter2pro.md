# What Is Scalping On Betting Exchanges?

## Mechanics
Scalping involves placing both a back bet and a lay bet on the same selection in quick succession, aiming to secure a profit of just one or two "ticks" (minimum odds increment). Modern software allows "greening up" to distribute gains evenly across all outcomes.

## Profitability & Realistic Expectations
"Success depends on skill, experience, risk appetite and market conditions." Professional traders can "grind out steady profits"; novices frequently experience mixed results.

Key profitability challenges:
- **Commission erosion**: Frequent trades accumulate exchange fees rapidly
- **Premium Charge**: Long-term Betfair winners face additional penalties
- **Execution risk**: Prices may move unfavorably before hedges complete
- **Low returns per trade**: Individual profits require scaling to become meaningful

## Essential Tools
- **Geeks Toy**: Most popular for scalping, designed for high-speed trading
- **BF Bot Manager**: Automates rapid order execution across Betfair, Betdaq, Matchbook

Software is "almost mandatory" for competitive scalping.

## Practical Recommendations
- Target liquid markets: UK horse racing within 20 minutes before races
- Maintain disciplined logs
- Use stop-losses
- Control emotions
- Profitability emerges "over hundreds of trades, not ten"
