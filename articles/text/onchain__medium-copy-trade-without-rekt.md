# How To Find The BEST Polymarket Wallets To Copy Trade

Only **12.7% of Polymarket users are actually profitable** — making smart wallet selection critical.

---

## 7 Wallet Types: Which to Follow, Which to Avoid

### AVOID These 6 Types:

**1. Crypto-bot wallets**
Profit from closing spreads. You can't replicate their edge — they already captured it. Copying them means you buy at market price after the bot captured the spread.

**2. Insider wallets**
Only profitable if identified before others find them. Once widely copied, edge disappears immediately.

**3. Low-liquidity traders**
You become exit liquidity when they leave positions.

**4. High-volatility gamblers**
Require millisecond-level bot infrastructure to compete.

**5. The "Gambloor"**
Large PnL from single lucky trades with terrible win rates overall. Look at the equity curve, not just total PnL.

**6. Fresh wallets with perfect records**
Often suspicious; some are documented to be intentionally deceptive (bait copy traders then trade opposite side).

---

### FOLLOW This Type:

**Basket traders with real edge:**
- Multiple strong wallets (~5–10) in same topic area
- Enter only when 80%+ agree on same outcome
- Consistent 60%+ win rate over 200+ trades
- 4+ month track record
- Niche expertise in 2–3 specific topic areas

---

## Evaluation Checklist (Before Copying Any Wallet)

- [ ] Win rate >60% across substantial trade sample
- [ ] Track record: at least 4 months
- [ ] Focus: 2–3 specific topic areas (not "generalist")
- [ ] <100 predictions per month
- [ ] Trades primarily in deep-liquidity markets
- [ ] Position building over days/weeks (not single trades)
- [ ] No recent performance decline (strategy decay check)

---

## The Basket Strategy (Best Practice)

1. Choose a category (politics, sports, crypto, economics)
2. Find 5–10 specialists using leaderboard + evaluation checklist
3. Verify they're NOT all copying each other
4. Only enter a position when **80%+ of the basket agrees** on the same outcome
5. Keep position within tight price band

This captures real-time consensus from multiple informed traders rather than betting on one person's judgment.

## Risk Factors

- **Front-running**: Prices move before your copy executes
- **Edge decay**: Previously profitable strategies stop working
- **Wash trading**: ~15% of Polymarket wallets show suspicious activity
- **Malicious tools**: Fake trading packages steal private keys
- **Counter-trading**: Some deliberately bait copy traders then trade opposite sides
