# The Anatomy of a Blockchain Prediction Market: Polymarket in the 2024 U.S. Presidential Election

## Abstract

The researchers analyzed Polymarket's 2024 U.S. Presidential Election market using on-chain Polygon data. They developed a transaction-level accounting framework with two components: a volume decomposition that separates exchange-equivalent turnover from share minting and burning, and trader-level disagreement measures.

## Key Findings

1. **Volume Discrepancy:** Naive aggregation showed $958M in October Trump-market volume, but the methodology reduced this to $391M — demonstrating the importance of proper accounting adjustments. Total reported volume of $3.6B conflates secondary trading with primary share issuance and redemption.

2. **Improved Market Quality:** The market showed significant efficiency gains, with arbitrage-deviation recovery times dropping from hours to under one minute, and Kyle's lambda declining from 0.53 to 0.01.

3. **Trading Behavior:** During October's large-account episode, capital flowed into both sides simultaneously, consistent with heterogeneous-beliefs trading rather than one-sided market manipulation.

4. **Intraday Seasonality:** The Trump YES market exhibited pronounced intraday seasonality concentrated in European and U.S. business hours.

5. **Broader Application:** The framework generalizes to other tokenized prediction markets beyond Polymarket.
