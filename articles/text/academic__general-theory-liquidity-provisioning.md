# A General Theory of Liquidity Provisioning for Prediction Markets

## Abstract

This paper introduces a general framework for liquidity provisioning for cost-function prediction markets with any finite set of securities. The framework is based on the idea of running several market makers "in parallel," showing formally that several notions of parallel market making are equivalent.

## Key Findings

- The paper establishes formal equivalence between different parallel market-making concepts
- The most general protocol allows LPs to submit an arbitrary cost function, which specifies their liquidity over the entire price space
- It highlights the inherent high dimensionality of liquidity provisioning
- The framework offers computationally feasible restricted protocols alongside the fully general version
- The work extends beyond existing two-asset DeFi protocols to support any number of assets
- Several existing DeFi protocols in the 2-asset case can be recovered through the model
