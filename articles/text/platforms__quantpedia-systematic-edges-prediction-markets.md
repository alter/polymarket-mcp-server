# Systematic Edges in Prediction Markets

QuantPedia analysis of systematic edges across prediction markets.

## Three Primary Profit Opportunities

### 1. Arbitrage Opportunities

**Inter-exchange arbitrage**: Exploits price differences across platforms (Polymarket, Kalshi, PredictIt). Opportunity exists when "the sum of the probabilities of opposing positions on different markets is less than 1 minus the transaction costs." Windows typically last seconds to minutes.

**Intra-exchange arbitrage**: Mispricings within single markets. When contract prices sum to less than $1, traders can buy all contracts and guarantee $1 payout. Conversely, prices exceeding $1 allow profitable selling.

Historical note: 2016 election on PredictIt showed arbitrage profits up to 55%; declined significantly by 2020.

### 2. Longshot Bias

Behavioral pattern: traders systematically overpay for underdogs. Research on 12,084 sports matches:
- Average profit after betting on **favorites**: -3.64%
- Average profit after betting on **outsiders**: -26.08%

Skilled traders exploit this by identifying true favorites undervalued relative to actual winning probabilities.

### 3. Other Approaches
- Trading strategies based on observing market dynamics
- AI tools analyzing data to identify high-probability outcomes (BetAI referenced)

## Current Limitations
Prediction market research landscape remains underdeveloped compared to established financial markets, limiting strategy refinement opportunities.
