# Sizing the Risk: Kelly, VIX, and Hybrid Approaches in Put-Writing on Index Options

**Author:** Maciej Wysocki  
**arXiv ID:** 2508.16598  
**Submitted:** August 9, 2025

## Abstract Overview

The research investigates "systematic put-writing strategies applied to S&P 500 Index options, with a focus on position sizing" as central to achieving strong long-term results. The author examines three distinct methodologies for determining position sizes: the Kelly criterion, VIX-based volatility adjustments, and an innovative hybrid combining both approaches.

## Key Findings

- **Strategy Focus:** Ultra-short-dated SPXW options (0-5 day expirations) targeting the volatility risk premium
- **Main Result:** The "hybrid sizing method consistently balances return generation with robust drawdown control, particularly under low-volatility conditions such as those seen in 2024"
- **Optimal Positioning:** Options positioned far out-of-the-money with very short timeframes deliver superior risk-adjusted performance

## Relevance

Compares Kelly against volatility-adjusted sizing (VIX) — an approach that can be adapted for prediction markets by replacing VIX with some measure of market uncertainty or calibration confidence.
