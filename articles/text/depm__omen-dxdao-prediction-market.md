# Omen: DXdao's Decentralized Prediction Market Platform

**Source:** Bitfwd / Medium

## Overview

Omen is a fully decentralized multi-chain prediction market platform built on the Gnosis Conditional Token Framework (CTF). It was initially developed by Gnosis and later managed by DXdao, making it a fully decentralized, multi-chain prediction market platform.

DXdao is global, pseudonymous, permissionless, and unstoppable, with no shareholders or CEO, functioning as an amorphous community building innovative products.

## How It Works

Users can establish prediction markets within minutes by specifying:
- An event and potential outcomes
- Resolution date and category
- Initial funding and arbitrator

Users deposit collateral (such as DAI stablecoins), which generates outcome tokens representing different possibilities.

Liquidity is enabled through a **fixed product market maker**, similar to how Uniswap markets work. This means liquidity is always available for users to trade as quickly as an Ethereum transaction. Unlike Uniswap:
- Fees are higher and collected separately rather than reinvested into liquidity
- The mechanism is extended to support an arbitrary number of outcome tokens trading in one market

## Outcome Resolution: Realit.io + Kleros

When markets reach their resolution date, **Realit.io** determines outcomes through a bonding mechanism where participants post collateral for their chosen outcome.

If disputed, **Kleros**—a decentralized arbitration protocol—selects randomized jurors to reach consensus through game-theoretic incentives. Kleros was integrated as a final arbitrator through Realit.io within the Conditional Token Framework.

The cross-collaboration between Gnosis, DXdao, Kleros, and Realit.io set a path forward for real-world prediction services.

## Kleros Integration Details

Kleros announced integration as a dispute resolution service with approximately $200,000 worth of GNO tokens placed under Kleros arbitration. Users can designate Kleros when creating prediction markets on Omen's interface.

A conditional grant: 10,000 GNO tokens available to DXdao if Gnosis Protocol trading volume from DXdao-controlled interfaces reaches $20,000,000. Kleros determines whether this milestone was satisfied.

## Business Model

- Fees directed toward liquidity providers initially
- Future plans: 10% of revenues retained as liquidity for the curve, 90% goes to DXdao treasury
- Supports multiple tokens through DXdao's Token Curated Registry

## Evolution: Presagio (Omen 2.0)

In 2024, **Presagio** emerged as "Omen 2.0," revitalizing the platform with a focus on AI-driven forecasting. Backed by a Gnosis DAO proposal (GIP-113), Presagio integrates GnosisAI agents, enabling advanced prediction strategies.

As of 2026, over 361 daily active AI agents execute more than 8.2 million transactions on Gnosis Chain, reflecting the rapid growth of autonomous, AI-driven market participation.

## Gnosis's Broader Evolution

Gnosis emerged alongside Ethereum's 2015 launch with a mission to coordinate information, belief, and value without intermediaries. From prediction markets, Gnosis expanded to:
- **Safe:** Smart contract wallet securing over $100 billion in assets
- **CoW Protocol:** Decentralized exchange saving users $500M+ in MEV losses
- **Gnosis Chain:** Proof-of-stake sidechain processing millions of daily transactions
- **Gnosis Pay:** Processing €32 million in EU transactions by 2025

Gnosis consolidated under one unified brand in July 2025.
