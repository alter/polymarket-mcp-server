# PredictIt Strategy, Edge & Trading Guide

## Platform Context
PredictIt: political prediction market, operated by Aristotle Inc. on behalf of Victoria University of Wellington (NZ). Founded 2014, operated under CFTC "No Action" letter for academic research. PredictIt shut down in 2024 after the CFTC withdrew its no-action letter. Kalshi inherited most of the audience.

## Core Strategies

### Informational Edge
Better information than the crowd is the most reliable path. Sources of edge:
- Domain expertise
- Speed advantages in processing information
- Quantitative modeling
- Primary research

The participant pool is smaller and less sophisticated than in financial markets, frequently driven by emotion.

### Target Niche, Less-Watched Markets
High-profile markets (presidential elections, major economic indicators) attract most attention and are priced efficiently. Edge lives in markets fewer people watch — thinner participation amplifies your research advantage.

### Arbitrage
- Cross-platform: Polymarket, Kalshi, PredictIt, sportsbooks
- Intra-platform: YES + NO sum < $1.00
- Historical: PredictIt showed arbitrage profits up to 55% in 2016 election; declined significantly by 2020

### Fading Overreactions to News
When major news breaks, prediction market prices whipsaw. Markets overreact to shocking/viral news — prices overshoot then mean-revert. If a market moves 15+ percentage points in under an hour on questionable information, assess fundamentals.

### Informed Trader Flow (Late-Money Strategy)
Informed traders place large positions as close to deadline as possible. Last-minute movements are driven by participants with superior information and are strongly predictive of outcomes.

### Conditional/Correlated Market Modeling
Model relationships between dependent markets. If Event A must happen for Event B, Event B's probability cannot exceed Event A's × conditional likelihood.

### Early Exit / Profit Taking
Don't hold every contract to expiration. If you bought YES at $0.30 and price moved to $0.65, selling now locks in $0.35 per contract (117% return) vs. holding for uncertain $1.00.

## Risk Management
- Position size: 2–5% of capital
- Only trade when new information clearly shifts probability by 10+ pp
- Diversify: 5–10 positions across at least 3 categories
- Never put 50%+ of bankroll in a single position
- Calibrate first: track 50 predictions without betting; 70%-confident predictions should come true ~70% of the time

## Realistic Earnings Expectations
- Beginners (0–6 months): -20% to +10%
- Advanced (18+ months): +30% to +150%
- Experts/professionals: +100% to +500%+
- ~70–80% of traders lose money overall

## Kelly Criterion
Position size = (bp − q) / b where b=odds, p=win probability, q=loss probability. Most traders use half-Kelly (reduce volatility).
