# The Liquidity Advantage of the Quote-Driven Market: Evidence from the Betting Industry

Academic paper on betting exchange market structure. Source: ScienceDirect (paywall; metadata only).

## Key Finding
While betting exchanges generally offer higher odds and bettor returns than bookmakers in **liquid markets**, the opposite is true in **illiquid markets** — a finding that rationalized Betfair's decision to start offering **quoted odds** in addition to exchange-based odds as of February 2013.

## Implication for Market Design
This finding explains the shift in financial market structures from pure quote-driven or pure order-driven structures to **hybrid structures** that combine advantages of both markets.

## Relevance to Prediction Markets
The same liquidity dynamics apply to modern prediction market CLOBs:
- In high-liquidity markets (major elections, Fed meetings): exchange/CLOB model is superior
- In low-liquidity markets (niche topics, early markets): AMM or quoted prices provide better execution

This is why platforms like Polymarket evolved from pure AMM to CLOB, and why Betfair is adding quoted-price layers to its exchange product.
