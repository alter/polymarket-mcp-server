# U.S. Soldier Charged With Using Classified Information to Profit from Prediction Market Bets on Maduro Capture

**Source:** CNBC, NPR, Fortune, ASIS, Axios, DOJ Press Release  
**Date:** April 23, 2026

## The Suspicious Bet

Just before the U.S. military carried out a controversial raid to capture Venezuelan President Nicolás Maduro in Caracas in January 2026, an anonymous user placed wagers on Polymarket betting that Maduro would be out of office by January 31, 2026. The account holder netted more than **$400,000** from Venezuela-related event contracts.

The user created the account in December, placed wagers only on four events — each related to the Venezuela operation — between Dec. 27 and Jan. 3, betting more than $32,000 that Maduro would be out by Jan. 31.

## The Perpetrator: U.S. Army Soldier Gannon Van Dyke

The Justice Department unsealed an indictment charging **Gannon Ken Van Dyke**, a U.S. Army soldier, with:
- Unlawful use of confidential government information for personal gain
- Theft of nonpublic government information
- Commodities fraud
- Wire fraud
- Making an unlawful monetary transaction

Van Dyke participated in the planning and execution of the U.S. military operation to capture Nicolás Maduro, called **"Operation Absolute Resolve,"** and used his access to classified information about that operation to personally profit.

**Profits:** More than $33,000 wagered across 13 bets yielded nearly **$410,000** in profit.

## Attempts to Conceal

After the operation, Van Dyke allegedly sent most of his proceeds to a **foreign cryptocurrency vault** before depositing them into a newly created online brokerage account. He asked Polymarket to delete his account, "falsely claiming that he had lost access to the email address."

## Regulatory First

This is the **first time the Commodity Futures Trading Commission** filed charges of insider trading in connection with event contracts.

CFTC Chairman Michael S. Selig stated: "The defendant was entrusted with confidential information about U.S. operations and yet took action that endangered U.S. national security and put the lives of American service members in harm's way."

## Polymarket's Response

Polymarket CEO Shayne Coplan stated the company "identified a user trading on classified government information, we referred the matter to the DOJ & cooperated with their investigation." Polymarket published enhanced market integrity rules to combat insider trading.

## Congressional Response

Rep. Ritchie Torres (NY-15) introduced the **Public Integrity in Financial Prediction Markets Act of 2026**, prohibiting federal officials and congressional staff from buying, selling, or exchanging prediction market contracts tied to government policy or political outcomes when they possess material nonpublic information.

## Broader Pattern

Suspicious Polymarket activities have been observed relating to:
- Russo-Ukrainian war events
- 2026 US strikes in Venezuela
- Israeli military operations (Israeli AF officer allegedly yielded $244,000)
- 2026 Israeli-US strikes on Iran (over 150 accounts correctly predicted attack's specific date)
- Interception: "the entire [squadron] is on Polymarket, the entire air force is betting"

## Regulatory Context

The CFTC has about **one-eighth the staff of the SEC** despite Kalshi alone receiving more than $2 billion in trades in a single week. In early 2024, monthly prediction market trading was ~$100 million; by late 2025 it had jumped to ~$13 billion.
