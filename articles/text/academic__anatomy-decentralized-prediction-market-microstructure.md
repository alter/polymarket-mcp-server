# The Anatomy of a Decentralized Prediction Market: Microstructure Evidence from the Polymarket Order Book

## Abstract

The research examines Polymarket's market structure using 30 billion order-book events across 52 days. The study identifies eight stylized facts about this on-chain prediction market, including a longshot spread premium and broad maker-wallet diversity with a concentrated tail.

## Key Findings

1. **Trade Direction Accuracy:** Inferring trade direction from public order-book feeds achieves only ~59% accuracy compared to on-chain records — significantly below the ~80% accuracy standard for traditional exchanges like Nasdaq.

2. **Microstructure Characteristics:** The research documents distinctive patterns including depth profiles closer to uniform distribution, maker diversity with concentrated activity, and effective spreads varying by market category.

3. **Self-Counterparty Trading:** Wash trades represent a median of 1% with a 22% upper tail, substantially lower than unregulated crypto venues (25-70%).

4. **Methodological Contribution:** Microstructure work on Polymarket requires trade direction to be sourced from on-chain OrderFilled events, not inferred from public feeds.

The microstructure of prediction markets has remained under-studied: limit-order-book depth profiles, spread decompositions, and the behaviour of liquidity providers near resolution were largely unobservable on early venues, which used scoring rules or sparse parimutuel pools.
