# Axioms for Constant Function Market Makers

**arXiv ID:** 2210.00048

## Overview

This paper develops axiomatic foundations for constant function market makers (CFMMs), complementing the axiomatic work of Frongillo et al. (arXiv:2302.00196) on the equivalence between CFMMs and prediction markets.

## Key Content

- Establishes formal axioms characterizing desirable properties of CFMMs
- Studies which axioms are necessary vs. sufficient for various CFMM designs
- Connects to the broader axiomatic tradition in mechanism design and prediction markets
- Relates CFMM axioms to scoring rule properties

## Relation to Prediction Markets

This work is part of the sequence establishing the formal connection between CFMMs (DeFi) and prediction markets (LMSR tradition): the axioms for good market-making are the same whether you're designing a DEX or a prediction market AMM.
