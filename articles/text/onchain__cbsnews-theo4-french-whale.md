# Theo4: The French Whale Who Made $82M on Polymarket

**Trader names:** Fredi9999, PrincessCaro, Theo4, Michie (same person)
**Total profit:** ~$82,320,102 (all Trump-related bets, 2024 election)
**Background:** Professional French investor and trader

---

## The Strategy: Neighbor Polling + Information Edge

### Step 1: Commission custom polling research
Hired **YouGov** to conduct battleground state polls in Pennsylvania, Michigan, and Wisconsin using "neighbor polling" — asking people who they think their *neighbors* are likely to vote for.

This captures "shy voters" who won't admit their own preference but will say what neighbors think — specifically designed for politically stigmatized candidates.

### Step 2: Before results, already $30M long
Even before engaging YouGov, he had already wagered **$30 million** on Trump based on his belief that national polls were skewed in favor of Harris.

### Step 3: All-in after polling results
Once he saw "neighbor question" results at end of October, he went all-in: used **11 different crypto accounts**, raised stake to **$80 million**, sold off virtually all liquid assets to fund the wager.

## Smart Money vs. Manipulation Debate

New York Times initially suggested the whale might be **manipulating public perception** by inflating Trump's odds. Theo4 refuted this, attributing his edge to personal research and data-driven approach.

Key question: Did the large position distort markets or reflect genuine information? Academic consensus: price distortions by whales are often corrected by counter-parties seeking the other side.
