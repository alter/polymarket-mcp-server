# Strategy-Proof Incentives for Predictions

## Abstract

The paper addresses a fundamental challenge in prediction mechanisms: designing systems that motivate agents to reveal predictions truthfully and promptly. While proper scoring rules work for myopic agents, they fail when participants consider long-term strategic effects. The author describes three mechanisms that achieve strategy-proofness even with non-myopic considerations.

## Key Findings

- The research identifies rules to distinguish between truthful and untruthful prediction settings, with extensive classification of continuous outcome scenarios
- One proposed mechanism restores prompt truthfulness where incumbent mechanisms fail
- The work addresses real-world applications in prediction markets and informal forums like analyst stock estimates, where deception and strategic delay commonly occur
