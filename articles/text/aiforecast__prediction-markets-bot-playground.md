# Prediction Markets Are Turning Into a Bot Playground

**Source:** Finance Magnates / Yahoo Finance
**URL:** https://www.financemagnates.com/trending/prediction-markets-are-turning-into-a-bot-playground/

## Key Facts

- 2025: prediction markets processed over $44B in trading volume (some estimates: $63.5B)
- 14 of 20 most profitable Polymarket wallets are bots
- AI agents represent >30% of Polymarket wallet activity
- 37% of agent wallets show positive PnL vs. 7-13% for human traders
- Arbitrage traders extracted ~$40M from Polymarket (April 2024 – April 2025) per IMDEA Networks Institute research
- One bot (Igor Mikerin's) generated $2.2M in 2 months using ensemble probability models + news/social data + continuous retraining

## Bot Architecture Described

- Ensemble probability models trained on news and social data
- Target: contracts undervalued vs. real-world probabilities
- Continuous model retraining
- Cross-platform arbitrage: Polymarket vs. Kalshi price discrepancies

## Relevance to Polymarket Trading

Market reality check: bots dominate Polymarket. The edge from LLM forecasting must beat other bots, not just humans. Speed arbitrage (CEX lag) is mostly saturated by high-frequency bots. The remaining edge is probabilistic: better calibrated long-horizon probability estimates.
