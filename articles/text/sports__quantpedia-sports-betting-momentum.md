# Sports Betting Used to Explain Value and Momentum Effects

**Source:** QuantPedia (summary of Moskowitz 2021)

## Core Framework

Sports betting markets serve as a clean laboratory for testing behavioral asset pricing theories because:

1. **No systematic risk** — bets are completely idiosyncratic, no aggregate risk premia
2. **Known resolution** — short contracts with known termination dates where uncertainty resolves independently of betting activity

This eliminates the **joint hypothesis problem** that confounds financial market tests.

## Key Behavioral Findings

### Price Movement from Open to Close
- Prices move "in a manner consistent with financial market evidence" for momentum and value signals
- Opening to close: momentum chasers and value seekers push prices away from fundamentals

### Reversal at Game Outcome
- Price movements **fully reverse** from close to game outcome
- Confirms: genuine mispricing rather than rational information incorporation

### Delayed Overreaction Theory Supported
- Bettors "follow momentum and value signals (e.g., chasing past performance and 'cheap' contracts) that push prices away from fundamentals"
- Consistent with delayed overreaction
- Inconsistent with underreaction and rational pricing

## Magnitude Comparison

| Market | Value/Momentum Return per Unit Risk |
|---|---|
| Sports betting | 1x (baseline) |
| Financial markets | ~5x |

Returns in sports betting are approximately **1/5** those found in financial markets per unit of risk.

## Why This Matters for Prediction Markets

The same momentum/value mechanism operates on Polymarket/Kalshi:
1. Early bettors push prices based on momentum (team on win streak, player in form)
2. Prices overshoot fundamental probabilities
3. As resolution approaches, mispricing corrects
4. Opportunity: fade momentum at open, or ride momentum and exit near close

**Transaction costs are decisive** — the small magnitude of sports betting anomalies (1/5 of financial markets) means vig/spread determines profitability. Low-fee venues (Polymarket sports at 0.03) are required for these small-edge strategies to be viable.
