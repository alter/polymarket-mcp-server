# Beating the House: Identifying Inefficiencies in Sports Betting Markets

## Abstract

The researchers developed an algorithm demonstrating that sports betting markets contain exploitable inefficiencies. Their work involved analyzing a novel dataset of bets, and created a non-parametric win probability model to find positive expected value situations across multiple leagues (NFL, NBA, NCAAF, NCAAB, WNBA).

## Key Findings

- The betting algorithm generated above-market returns across five major sports betting markets
- Markets exhibit identifiable inefficiencies allowing consistent outperformance
- The research is framed as increasingly relevant following the 2018 Supreme Court decision removing federal sports betting restrictions
- Relates prediction market efficiency to the Efficient Markets Hypothesis (Fama 1970)
